# What a user's corrections buy; doc-split-v1 as the laptop segmenter (W46.seg-adapt-curve)

Generated by `scripts/segbench/seg_adapt_curve.py` (method in its docstring) from cached answers and doc-split-v1's cached page embeddings (`doc_split.py`, onnxruntime on the Mac CPU). No calls, no money. Document gaps only; a gap beside a label photo is a boundary by construction.

## Findings (2026-10-23)

1. **doc-split-v1 is not the laptop segmenter.**
   - **Zero-shot it under-splits every box.** Pages in an exact document are 8.2-42.5% on the owner boxes, 0.0% on Lincoln (4 of 80 boundaries found) and 18.0% on CREST.
   - **Its boundary logits do rank boundaries.** AUC is 0.87 on Lincoln and 0.91 on CREST. The miss is calibration, not ranking.
   - **Refit on the development boxes, it still trails the 2B.** The refit covers the six head and CRF parameters plus the beta calibration. It reaches 21.8-61.3% on the owner boxes, 31.0% on Lincoln and 27.3% on CREST.
   - **On the test boxes, under options.md's rule, it does no better than checking every gap.** It reaches 28.5% unchecked, and its fitted flag level checks every gap (86.8 per 100 pages). The 2B gets 78.8% after 78.8 checks.
   - **As a disagreement partner it flags too much.** It disagrees with the 2B on 11-28 gaps per 100 pages.
   - The laptop profile keeps the corpus-trained 2B.
2. **A few labels buy little beyond the gaps they correct, and they can hurt.**
   - **Stanton.** The 2B's threshold-and-prior refit lowers held-out errors from 11.3 to 9.3-9.6 per 100 gaps for k = 25 to 200. Pages exact moves less than a point beyond what the corrected gaps give.
   - **Lincoln.** At k=25 the same refit takes pages exact from 38.9% to 53.3% and errors from 18.0 to 14.8. It then flattens at about 15 errors whatever k is. The over-splits that remain are confident ones, which no threshold reaches.
   - **Boxes the 2B already does well.** The refit is flat or harmful. On CREST at k=25 pages exact falls from 92.4% to 79.6% while held-out errors barely move (3.2 to 3.4): the gaps it flips merge long documents.
   - **What follows for the kit.** A refit must be scored on the box's held-out gaps and kept only when it helps, which matches A §4's design.
   - **doc-split-v1's per-box refit.** It gains most (Dean 44.9% to 75.4% at k=100) and still stays far below the 2B.
3. **The review screen's rule is disagreement with a second model, not the 2B's own confidence.**
   - **The 2B's confidence is close to random on the hard boxes.** Checks per 100 pages to reach 98%:

     | Box | 2B confidence | random |
     |---|---|---|
     | Stanton | 87.6 | 91.2 |
     | Lincoln | 96.2 | 95.1 |

   - **Against the 27B teacher (local on a 32 GB Mac).** Checks to 98% are 8.9 (Dean), 18.1 (Deaver), 5.0 (Herrnstein), 76.8 (Stanton) and 2.3 (CREST). On Lincoln it fails (96.2), because the teacher over-splits the same manuscripts.
   - **Against the cloud partner it is best or tied everywhere.** Lincoln needs 54.8 checks (Claude), Stanton 76.8 (Gemini), Dean 8.4.
   - **At 10 checks per 100 pages on Stanton:**

     | Rule | Pages exact |
     |---|---|
     | Against the cloud partner | 90.5% |
     | Against the teacher | 86.0% |
     | 2B confidence | 80.7% |
     | Random | 75.0% |

   - **A Vision page-kind change adds little.** Over the 2B's confidence it helps only on Deaver and Stanton at small budgets.
4. **Not run here.**
   - **The LoRA continuation of the 2B.** Judged nice-to-have at its Sherlock review and filed as W46.seg-adapt-lora. The cheap refit leaves Lincoln's confident over-splits, but one 260-gap box cannot measure a 200-gap LoRA, and a cloud voter already settles the route there. The useful form of the test needs a second box of a Lincoln-like material.
   - **Few-shot examples in the window prompt.** These are W46.seg-adapt-curve-paid (gateway).

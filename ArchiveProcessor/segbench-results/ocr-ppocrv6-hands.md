## Fine-tuned on one hand

Training lines are the hand's own truth lines, paired to blla lines by a monotone alignment on the base model's reading (character error at most 0.6, lengths within 2.0x); 10 pool lines pick the epoch (early stopping); scored on held-out pages read on blla lines, tuned against the base model on the same lines. owner: 77 of 88 truth lines paired over 4 pages; blackwell: 1004 of 1386 truth lines paired over 40 pages.

| training | lines | device | minutes | peak GB | held-out pages | base | tuned | delta | interval |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| blackwell-n20-cpu | 20 | cpu | 6.9 | 3.56 | 16 | 59.7 | 59.6 | -0.14 | -0.84 to +0.60 |

Training: ketos (kraken 7.1.1), batch 1, AdamW at 0.0001 constant, 6 threads, fp32, at most 1500 line-steps or 25 epochs, early stopping after 5 epochs with a lag of 3 (blackwell-n20 ran at 10 and 5 before that change; its best epoch, 2, is the same under either). The PP-OCRv6 config's own optimizer, AdamW+Muon, runs Muon's Newton-Schulz step in bfloat16, which has no fast CPU kernel (97% of a step, on one core); a batch of 8 passed 8.8 GB within 17 s (a first attempt at blackwell-n20, the one stopped in the guard figures below). The owner's rows leave one of the four Shils pages out and train on the other three pages' lines; their truth is the drafted owner truth (hand places owner-checked), so a gain there is agreement with the drafters, not a ranking (OCR-1).

The CPU trainings use kraken's torch.compile of the forward pass; the MPS one runs eagerly (TORCHDYNAMO_DISABLE=1), because Inductor's Metal kernel for this model asks for 32,896 bytes of threadgroup memory, more than the GPU allows, and `ketos train -d mps` fails at its first step. 
Blackwell n240 did not train this session: CPU trainings run one at a time (two side by side each ran about 4x slower) and the longer ones did not fit the session.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** Public L1-L3: the sources' own transcriptions (By the People crowd text, Bentham Transcribe), not drafted by any scored route. Owner pages: drafted by Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash, the hand places checked blind by the owner (W46.ocr-hands-rescore); typed and mixed lines not re-checked, so Gemini 3.8 Flash's rows there are agreement with its own draft. Blackwell fine-tune truth: By the People's transcriptions of the volume. No line was drawn or corrected by a Claude subagent.
- **Selection.** The 895's frozen L1-L3 strata; the owner's pages by the panel's kind label; the Blackwell volume pages by a seeded shuffle of the volume's pages with at least 8 truth lines, none in the 895; the owner's fine-tune hand is the folder with most hand pages. None chosen by a tested route.
- **Comparison.** Every row reads the same pages; comparator readings are the cached ones (`ocr_rescore.py` sources), scored by `ocr_panel_score.score` with the stratum's settings. A route that did not read every page is left out of a table rather than scored on fewer.
- **Noise.** kraken on CPU is deterministic, so the rerun floor of our rows is 0; the comparators' rerun floor is ocr-rerun-noise.md's 0.49 points. A paired interval across 0 is a tie. Fine-tune rows rest on few held-out pages (one per fold for the owner's hand), so their intervals are wide.
- **Scope.** Line recognition only: placement of these lines is W46.place-hands. The Qwen few-line prompting arm (cluster) and the Gemini arm (W46.ocr-ppocrv6-hands-paid) are not here.

# kraken PP-OCRv6 as the laptop handwriting route (W46.ocr-ppocrv6-hands)

Generated by `scripts/segbench/ocr_ppocrv6_hands.py report` from saved readings. Kiessling's kraken port of PP-OCRv6 medium (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21788410, kraken 7.1.1, CPU) read on two line sources: kraken blla baselines (`ppocrv6-kraken-blla`) and Apple Vision's line boxes as rectangles (`ppocrv6-kraken-vision`); `apple-vision (lines)` is Vision's own text of those lines (the cached Vision page readings do not cover L1-L3); then fine-tuned on corrected lines of one hand. `ppocrv6-medium` is the native PaddleOCR pipeline (its own detector) from FarmShare. Mean NES in points; a missing or empty reading scores 0. No money; no text in the repo.

## Verdict

- **On blla lines kraken PP-OCRv6 is the best line recogniser measured here, and not the laptop handwriting pick.** 76.8 on the public L1-L3 against 62.1 for the native PaddleOCR PP-OCRv6 pipeline (+14.7 (+11.7 to +17.7)) and 61.5 for Apple Vision's own text of its lines, but -9.4 (-11.3 to -7.5) behind LightOnOCR-3 4B (86.2). On the owner's 16 handwritten pages 71.6 against 89.9 for LightOnOCR-3 4B 8-bit on the Mac (agreement with the drafters, OCR-1). It needs 2-6 GB a page call on the CPU, so it is the low-memory fallback, not the default.
- **The line source matters more than the reader:** on Vision's line boxes the same model scores 66.4 (-10.5 (-13.1 to -7.9) against blla), losing most on the hands (L1 57.6 against 74.1); typed L3 is level.
- **Fine-tuning on one public hand gains little before 80 lines:** 20 lines cpu -0.1 (-0.8 to +0.6); 40 lines cpu -0.3 (-1.1 to +0.5); 80 lines cpu +1.1 (+0.3 to +1.9); 80 lines mps +1.5 (+0.7 to +2.3); 160 lines cpu +2.5 (+1.7 to +3.2); 160 lines mps +2.6 (+1.7 to +3.4); 240 lines cpu +2.2 (+1.4 to +3.1); 240 lines mps +2.9 (+2.0 to +3.7) on 16 held-out Blackwell pages, base 59.7.
- **The curve past 80 lines, paired on the same held-out pages:** 160 minus 80 lines (cpu) +1.4 (+1.0 to +1.8); 240 minus 80 lines (cpu) +1.1 (+0.3 to +1.8); 160 minus 80 lines (mps) +1.1 (+0.7 to +1.5); 240 minus 80 lines (mps) +1.3 (+0.9 to +1.7). These intervals describe 16 held-out Blackwell pages: 12 same-volume and four other-volume pages; training-seed variability is unmeasured.
- **Kit recommendation.** Target about 160 corrected lines for an optional same-hand fine-tune, using eager MPS when available. 80 to 160 lines pays on this hand on both backends; 240 against 160 is a measured tie (-0.27 (-1.13 to +0.51) CPU; +0.25 (-0.39 to +0.74) MPS): every n160 and n240 training stopped early at 5 epochs, under its step ceiling, so both sizes trained to the same early stop. Require line-broken corrected truth and a separate held-out acceptance check for each hand. One hand and one seed do not justify making fine-tuning the default handwriting route.
- **On the owner's Shils pages it made 3 of 4 held-out pages worse** (-4.2, -3.9, +3.0, -17.3). Their truth is one paragraph a page, so the training lines are word runs matched to blla lines by the base model's reading (mean character error 0.18-0.33 on the kept lines), and three pages are too few; this does not show the owner's hand cannot be learnt, only that paragraph truth paired this way does not train it. A kit fine-tune needs line-broken, corrected lines.

The line sets are nested prefixes of one seeded order, with the same ten validation lines and 16 held-out Blackwell pages (12 same-volume and four other-volume pages), disjoint from the training and validation pages. The step ceiling allows n160 9 epochs and n240 6 (1500 line-steps at most), but all 4 n160 and n240 trainings stopped early at 5 epochs (`tunes[].epochs`), so the ceiling never bound: both sizes trained to the same early stop, and 240 against 160 lines is measured, not cut short. Only this same-hand Blackwell holdout is scored for the larger tunes: the previous public L1-L3 and owner zero-shot panels remain above; the new tunes make no full-895, full-96 or cross-hand claim.

Training: ketos (kraken 7.1.1), batch 1, AdamW at 0.0001 constant, 6 threads, fp32, at most 1500 line-steps or 25 epochs, early stopping after 5 epochs with a lag of 3 (blackwell-n20 ran at 10 and 5 before that change; its best epoch, 2, is the same under either). The PP-OCRv6 config's own optimizer, AdamW+Muon, runs Muon's Newton-Schulz step in bfloat16, which has no fast CPU kernel (97% of a step, on one core); a batch of 8 passed 8.8 GB within 17 s (a first attempt at blackwell-n20, stopped by the guard). The owner's rows leave one of the four Shils pages out and train on the other three pages' lines; their truth is the drafted owner truth (hand places owner-checked), so a gain there is agreement with the drafters, not a ranking (OCR-1).

The CPU trainings use kraken's torch.compile of the forward pass; the MPS trainings run eagerly (TORCHDYNAMO_DISABLE=1), because Inductor's Metal kernel for this model asks for 32,896 bytes of threadgroup memory, more than the GPU allows, and `ketos train -d mps` fails at its first step. blackwell-n80-mps: 40 s an epoch against 125 on the CPU (each beside no other training; 5 and 10 epochs before early stopping, so 3.3 and 20.9 minutes), tuned 61.3 against 60.9, not identical. blackwell-n160-mps: 76 s an epoch against 217 on the CPU (each beside no other training; 5 and 5 epochs before early stopping, so 6.3 and 18.1 minutes), tuned 62.3 against 62.2, not identical. blackwell-n240-mps: 112 s an epoch against 317 on the CPU (each beside no other training; 5 and 5 epochs before early stopping, so 9.3 and 26.4 minutes), tuned 62.6 against 62.0, not identical.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** Public L1-L3: the sources' own transcriptions (By the People crowd text, Bentham Transcribe), not drafted by any scored route. Owner pages: drafted by Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash; on the 16 handwritten pages the hand places were checked blind by the owner (114 places, W46.ocr-hands-rescore); the mixed pages' lines and any typed lines were not re-checked, so Gemini 3.8 Flash's rows there are agreement with its own draft. The three agreement-only letters (p086, p094, p096) have truth no one adjudicated and are not scored. Blackwell fine-tune truth: By the People's transcriptions of the volume. No line was drawn or corrected by a Claude subagent.
- **Selection.** The 895's frozen L1-L3 strata; the owner's pages by the panel's kind label, less the three agreement-only letters; the Blackwell volume pages by a seeded shuffle of the volume's pages with at least 8 truth lines, none in the 895; the owner's fine-tune hand is the folder with most hand pages. Page selection is not by a tested route. Training-line pairing and filtering do use the base reader's text, so harder unpaired lines are underrepresented; every held-out page is still scored in full.
- **Comparison.** Every row reads the same pages; comparator readings are the cached ones (`ocr_rescore.py` sources), scored by `ocr_panel_score.score` with the stratum's settings. A route that did not read every page is left out of a table rather than scored on fewer. This regeneration includes the already-shipped Nemotron-Parse 2.0 and Unlimited-OCR caches (W46.ocr-new-open-boxes), and Granite's completed Mac run (W46.ocr-new-open-boxes, 2026-11-01), which leaves 7 of the 18 scored owner pages empty; ocr-hands-rescore.md scores the same readings since W46.hands-kit-epochs (before it, a set from before the Mac run completed, with 10 of the 16 hand pages failed). No comparator model was rerun for the curve extension.
- **Noise.** Fixed-weight kraken CPU inference has an expected rerun floor of 0 (deterministic inference; not remeasured in this extension); the comparators' rerun floor is ocr-rerun-noise.md's 0.49 points. The interval decides: a paired difference is a tie when its 95% bootstrap interval reaches 0 or the difference is inside the rerun floor, whatever the p. The sign-flip p is shown beside it; at n = 18 the two can disagree near the edge (ppocrv6-kraken-blla vs apple-vision +9.9 (+0.9 to +19.2, p 0.06); ppocrv6-kraken-blla vs ppocrv6-kraken-vision +9.1 (+0.8 to +18.3, p 0.06)), and such a pair is a resolved but marginal lead. Fine-tune intervals are exploratory and unadjusted across contrasts; they bootstrap the held-out pages, not training seeds or hands: seed/device training variability is unmeasured (one seed, one training per condition). One held-out owner page per fold cannot estimate population uncertainty.
- **Scope.** Line recognition only: placement of these lines is W46.place-hands. The Qwen few-line prompting arm (W46.ocr-ppocrv6-hands-qwen, cluster) and the Gemini arm (W46.ocr-ppocrv6-hands-paid) are separate reports. The 160/240-line extension is W46.ocr-ppocrv6-hands-curve. One public hand and one owner folder: whether other hands gain alike is not settled. MPS against CPU is one training per size/device, eager against compiled, so it gives speed, not equivalence.

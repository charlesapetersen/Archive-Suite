### Reading

- **On the test boxes, 64.0% of the 2B's errors and 90.5% of the 4B's are the teacher's.** The 2B shares 16 of its 25 wrong gaps with the teacher, the 4B 19 of 21. That is co-occurrence in single runs, not a ceiling: it does not show that training on this teacher could not remove them, nor what a better teacher or owner-ruled labels would gain. Only a training run scored on held-out gaps measures that.
- **On the public boxes, material none of them trained on, the split is 36 of 49 (2B) and 29 of 32 (4B).** Lincoln and CREST differ from the owner's corpus in kind. The overlap there is descriptive too: it does not forecast how much a second-family teacher would help a user whose material is unlike the owner's.

## The held-out set size

Paired document bootstrap over the five owner boxes (463 documents, 807 gaps): documents drawn with replacement until the draw holds N gaps, 4,000 draws a size, seed 46. Half-width of the 95% interval of the difference, in points; 'needed' is the size whose half-width reaches 3 points, extrapolated from the 1,000-gap figure by 1/√N. Review load is gaps flagged at confidence ≤ 0.85 per 100 pages.

| difference | measure | 100 gaps | 300 gaps | 1,000 gaps | needed for ±3 points | observed at 1,000 (interval) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 4B minus 2B | pages exact | ±13.79 | ±8.15 | ±4.4 | 2,151 | -4.23 to 4.58 |
| 4B minus 2B | gap accuracy | ±3.94 | ±2.16 | ±1.2 | 160 | -0.3 to 2.1 |
| 4B minus 2B | flagged per 100 pages | ±3.81 | ±2.2 | ±1.19 | 157 | -3.04 to -0.66 |
| 27B teacher minus 2B | pages exact | ±13.54 | ±7.78 | ±4.22 | 1,979 | -4.21 to 4.23 |
| 27B teacher minus 2B | gap accuracy | ±5.0 | ±3.0 | ±1.65 | 302 | -1.6 to 1.7 |
| 27B teacher minus 2B | flagged per 100 pages | ±4.33 | ±2.4 | ±1.34 | 200 | -2.77 to -0.09 |

### Reading

- **A 3-point gate on pages exact needs about 2,151 held-out gaps** of owner-like material; at 300 gaps the interval is ±8.15 points and at 100 ±13.79. Gap accuracy needs fewer (about 160), because a gap is a smaller unit than a document's pages.
- **The rerun floor is on top of this.** The audit measured 1.4-3.2 points a box between identical reruns (SEG-10). The gate's interval covers sampling the held-out set; a model's own wobble at temperature 0 under vLLM is a second error of about the same size, so a 3-point difference is only separated when both are inside it: the gate should score two runs, or demand the difference clear the larger floor.
- **These are owner gaps, and the kit's user has tags.** The interval is sampling only; a gate scored against a user's tags inherits their noise (5.3% wrong among agreed labels, interval 0-12.4%, `label-clean.md`). A wrong tag cancels in a paired difference only where the two models agree; at a gap where one is right and the other wrong it reverses which one scores, moving the difference by two gaps. That rate was measured on agreed labels and is not located on the discordant gaps, so the table below is a sensitivity, not a measured noise term: the shift in the gap-accuracy difference if that share of the discordant gaps' tags were wrong, every one against the observed winner.

| difference | known gaps | discordant | observed gap-accuracy difference | shift at 5.3% | shift at 12.4% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4B minus 2B | 807 | 31 | +0.87 points | ±0.41 | ±0.95 |
| 27B teacher minus 2B | 807 | 39 | +0.12 points | ±0.51 | ±1.2 |

### The minimum support, on the gate's own resampling (W46.kit-gate-groups)

Until 2026-11-11 it resampled folders only, so a held-out set of one folder (one box, as the continuation's next box is) had an interval of zero width: a candidate 2.96 points worse on 270 gaps passed with one folder and failed with 30 (measurement review pass 12, P12-1). #### Reading

- **The gate's minimum stays 267 known gaps.** On the gate's innermost unit, documents, the larger pair (27B teacher against the 2B) needs 271 gaps at seed 46 and 271-314 over seeds 46-50: the document bootstrap above, which drew documents to a size, gave the same figure. The floor is derived on documents because they are the unit every held-out set has, a one-box set included; below it the interval is too narrow to trust.
- **Between boxes the interval widens, and the gate reads that from the interval, not the floor.** Resampled as five folders the same gaps need 504 for ±3 points (235 for the 4B pair), because the boxes differ from one another. A held-out set of several unlike folders gets that wider interval, and a trailing candidate fails on it; the floor only stops a set too small for any interval.
- **One box alone varies.** The boxes' own figures run from 39 to 546: a box with a few long documents where the models differ has a wide interval at its size. The gate scores the box it is given; the floor is a minimum, not a promise that 267 gaps of any box reach ±3 points.
- **A held-out set of one document is refused.** The draw then repeats that document, and no number of its gaps gives an interval.

### The minimum number of documents (W46.kit-gate-docs)

Until 2026-11-11 the gate refused one held-out document and accepted two. With two, the document draw has three outcomes, so the interval's low end is about the worse document's mean, and a candidate passes whenever both documents score above -3 (measurement review pass 13, P13-2). The table is simulated, not measured: one folder of 280 known gaps (about the gate's floor) in equal documents, the shipped model right on 95% of them, and the candidate's true paired difference varying from document to document with the spread given; each cell is the share of 1000 simulated boxes on which `train_seg.py check`'s paired interval (1,000 draws) has its low end above -3, so a candidate worse than the gate allows passes. | 1.5 points | -4.5 points | 10.3% | 2.3% | 1.3% | 0.8% | 0.7% | 0.6% |

#### Reading

- **The gate needs at least 10 held-out documents.** With a 3-point spread, a candidate 3.5 points worse passes 24.8% of the time on two documents and 9.6-9.8% on three to five; from ten it passes 4.8%, and 3.8-4.7% at 20-30, so more documents do not lower it much: what is left is set by the number of gaps, which the 267-gap floor governs. Below the minimum the gate refuses with the count, as it refuses below the known-gap floor, and the manifest states the folders, documents and gaps the held-out figure stands on.
- **The spread is not measured.** No per-document spread of a paired difference is committed; between the five owner boxes it is about 1.5 points after sampling variance, so 3 points is the harder plausible case. At 1.5 points two documents still pass the 3.5-point candidate 25.5% of the time, against 4.2% at ten.
- **Owner-like boxes are far above it.** They hold about 1.8 gaps a document (809 in 441), so a held-out set at the 267-gap floor has about 150 documents. The minimum bites on a box of a few long documents, such as bound volumes or registers, the case the continuation's next box may be.

## The gate: `train_seg.py check` and the manifest

`train_seg.py check` (institutional-compute) is the kit's held-out gate. It reads `cluster_seg.py` answers only, scores each run's per-gap calls on the held-out collections against their tags (and against the owner's rulings when a file of them is given) with a bootstrap of folders and, inside each, documents (W46.kit-gate-groups), and on a locked public anchor box (pages exact, gap accuracy, the review load to 98%) with a document bootstrap, each paired against the shipped model. It writes H §6.3's manifest: kit version, prompt id, base build, weights, adapter and training-data hashes, the scores with intervals, the Mac figures and the gate's verdict, and exits 1 when the gate fails. The rule, as decided (call 7.2, 2026-11-06; enforced by W46.kit-gate-rule): two scored runs, each passing; in each, an anchor score, a held-out score on at least 267 known gaps in at least 10 documents (the larger of the two measured "needed" figures above, 147 and 267, derived again on the gate's own resampling, and the documents simulated above, W46.kit-gate-docs; the owner's rulings when they reach that, else the tags), a paired held-out gap-accuracy interval against the shipped model whose low end is above -3 points, over every gap the shipped run answered with the candidate's unanswered gaps counted wrong, and anchor pages exact less than 3.2 points (the top of the pages-exact rerun floor) below the shipped run's. A candidate with no shipped run, no paired gap or no paired anchor fails (W46.kit-gate-coverage). The pages-exact rerun floor is not reused as a gap-accuracy tie unit (P7-4): the second run stands in for rerun wobble.

The app reads the manifest as `archive-seg-manifest.json` beside the weights. `LocalSegmenter.refusal` runs the shipped build as its own, and refuses any other Model setting that is not a folder on the Mac holding a manifest of kit `seg-kit-1`, prompt c1, with an anchor pages-exact score: it is returned before a run starts (`WindowStageTransport.current`), as an unknown voter is refused today. Six checks in `SegmentationEngineTestDriver` cover it.

The shipped build's own manifest is `seg-kit-manifest-shipped.json`: weights `e4e00067fe02…` (the 8-bit MLX build), anchor Lincoln 33.07% of pages exact (20.96 to 47.83), gap accuracy 82.49%, review load to 98% 95.4 gaps per 100 pages, on 75 resolved documents (251 pages) with 3 unsure gaps unscored, against truth `5ba5a0e67380…`. Its anchor answers are the full-precision FarmShare run, and its held-out run is not on this Mac, so its held-out block is empty and its own gate says so; the app requires only the anchor and the prompt.

## The kit end to end, dry (2026-10-31)

Each step run as far as it can go without a cluster (no engine session contacts Sherlock) and without training on this Mac (W46.kit-mac-lora measures that):

| step | how it was run | result |
|---|---|---|
| training set | `train_seg.py data`, offline test on a synthetic pack (`test_institutional_compute.Check`) | only whole agreed training windows train, held-out never; start-trust keeps more |
| train and merge | the README's `slurm_args` recipe against the example profile, printed not submitted | one sbatch line for `train.sbatch` (train, then merge) |
| held-out and anchor answers | `submit.sh seg seg-student --pack … --dry-run` (example profile) | one sbatch line serving the merged student under its name; the packs come from `corpus_pairs.py subpack --split holdout` and `public_segmentation.py pack` |
| the gate | `train_seg.py check` on the cached FarmShare runs (Lincoln; the joint 2B's held-out run) | reproduced the bench's figures at the time (4B 46.7%, 2B 34.9% on Lincoln, before the owner's rulings were folded; the manifest above is on the current truth); joint 2B 80.4% of 7,277 held-out tag gaps |
| the app | `LocalSegmenter.refusal` on the written manifests | the shipped manifest passes; no manifest, no anchor score or another prompt is refused |

What the dry run found missing from the kit:

- **No step builds the Mac model.** The 8-bit MLX build was made by the Vision OCR lab's `convert-mlx.py` by hand (`seg-students.md` §On the Mac); the kit stops at a merged model on the cluster. A user needs a `train_seg.py` or kit step that converts and quantises it, then hashes what the app will load.
- **The manifest has to be copied beside the Mac build by hand**, and its weights hash is of whatever folder `--weights` named: hashed on the cluster's merged model, it does not match the Mac build.
- **Mac figures are not measured by the kit.** H §6.3 asks for the kit's own warm-up run; `check` takes them from a file (`--mac`), and nothing writes that file yet.
- **The local copy of the shipped build has no manifest** (`~/.local/share/visionocr-ocrlab/mlx/`): a Model setting naming that folder is now refused until `seg-kit-manifest-shipped.json` is copied there as `archive-seg-manifest.json`. The default setting (the published repository) is unaffected.
- The plan-route arm (a `claude -p` voter in the kit) is held for the gateway (CLAUDE.md, "Plan routes wait for the gateway"), so the coding-agent leg was not run through `claude -p`. The five-hour window went from 26% to 39% over this session, which shares it with anything else running; the kit's own steps were seconds of CPU.

The first three are closed by `train_seg.py mac` (W46.kit-mac-build, 2026-11-11): it converts and quantises the merged student, times a warm-up and writes `archive-seg-mac.json` in the build; `check --weights BUILD` then writes the manifest there, its `mac` block from that file, its weights hash of the build with the two kit files left out (the shipped build still hashes to the shipped manifest's `e4e00067…` with a figures file beside it). Run here on the shipped 8-bit 2B (`--build`) with nine Deaver windows at 1,024 px (the first untimed): 18.2 s a window, 6.2 s a page, 4.2 GB peak, 2.5 GB, 9.6 bits a weight, 9 of 9 parsed; `seg-students.md` §On the Mac has 18 s and 4.4 GB on the three development boxes, so one warm-up reproduces the bench's figure to within its rounding (a single run, no rerun floor). The convert half was run on the untrained Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B (no merged student is on this Mac; they are in the owner's backup): it gives the shipped build's shape (2,213M parameters, 9.6 bits a weight, 2.5 GB) and reads coherent text, but answers 1 of 5 windows in a form that parses (it writes a cue per gap and runs past the limit), which is what training teaches and why `mac` exits 1 then.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The owner's ruled boxes (Dean, Deaver, Herrnstein; test boxes Stanton and RG165), Stanton with the owner's 2026-10-31 segmentation-sheet rulings folded (W46.seg-truth-rescore); the agent-audited public truths of Lincoln and CREST with the owner's rulings folded (Lincoln 5 ruled, 3 unsure, `5ba5a0e67380…`, CREST 6 ruled, 4 unsure, `c7a0cfcac896…`); their other gaps remain agent-audited. Unsure gaps are out of every count (W46.seg-unsure-mask). Dating: the owner's year tags.
- **Selection.** Whole boxes, chosen before these models existed; the dating 131 by whether the year is in the ABBYY text, which is how they were chosen in W41.date-model (ABBYY is not an input here).
- **Comparison.** Every model on the same windows and the same request (`method_local.request`, the images at 1,024 px, temperature 0, thinking off), each on one L40S under vLLM 0.31, read from `method_openrouter.py`'s caches; the untrained 4B was run on the development boxes only.
- **Noise.** The bootstrap's intervals above are sampling only; the rerun floor (1.4-3.2 points a box) is not in them, and truth noise enters only as the discordant-gap sensitivity. Shared-error counts are single runs, and descriptive. The minimum number of documents is a simulation, not a measurement: its per-document spread is assumed (3 and 1.5 points), and each rate is over 1,000 simulated boxes, about ±1.4 points at 5%.
- **Scope.** The owner's material and two public boxes. Overlap is not a training ceiling and forecasts no other teacher. Accuracy on owner-ruled held-out gaps (the checkpack and Sasha) is in `seg-kit-gate-sherlock.md`.

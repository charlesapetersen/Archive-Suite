### Reading

- **On the test boxes, 58.6% of the 2B's errors and 82.6% of the 4B's are the teacher's.** The 2B shares 17 of its 29 wrong gaps with the teacher, the 4B 19 of 23. Those cannot be trained away with this teacher: a better teacher, or owner-ruled labels, is what would move them. The rest are errors the students add on top, which more or better-matched training could reach.
- **On the public boxes, material none of them trained on, the split is 36 of 50 (2B) and 30 of 35 (4B).** Lincoln and CREST differ from the owner's corpus in kind, so how much of the students' loss there comes from the teacher says how far a second-family teacher could help a user whose material is unlike the owner's.
- **Not yet on the owner's 80-gap checkpack or Sasha.** No route has answers on the windows those gaps sit in (the checkpack's gaps are 71 in training collections and 9 held out; Sasha was locked on 2026-10-30), so the students' accuracy on owner-ruled held-out gaps, and the split there, wait for the teacher-and-students job W46.seg-boxes-score prepares for Sherlock (this item's teacher arm is shared with it, as the audit asks).

## The held-out set size

Paired document bootstrap over the five owner boxes (463 documents, 809 gaps): documents drawn with replacement until the draw holds N gaps, 4,000 draws a size, seed 46. Half-width of the 95% interval of the difference, in points; 'needed' is the size whose half-width reaches 3 points, extrapolated from the 1,000-gap figure by 1/√N. Review load is gaps flagged at confidence ≤ 0.85 per 100 pages.

| difference | measure | 100 gaps | 300 gaps | 1,000 gaps | needed for ±3 points | observed at 1,000 (interval) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 4B minus 2B | pages exact | ±13.33 | ±7.87 | ±4.23 | 1,988 | -5.4 to 3.07 |
| 4B minus 2B | gap accuracy | ±3.5 | ±2.17 | ±1.2 | 160 | -0.1 to 2.3 |
| 4B minus 2B | flagged per 100 pages | ±3.85 | ±2.33 | ±1.23 | 168 | -2.94 to -0.48 |
| 27B teacher minus 2B | pages exact | ±13.67 | ±7.79 | ±4.34 | 2,093 | -3.42 to 5.27 |
| 27B teacher minus 2B | gap accuracy | ±5.0 | ±2.99 | ±1.55 | 267 | -0.8 to 2.3 |
| 27B teacher minus 2B | flagged per 100 pages | ±4.35 | ±2.57 | ±1.43 | 227 | -2.76 to 0.09 |

### Reading

- **A 3-point gate on pages exact needs about 1,988 held-out gaps** of owner-like material; at 300 gaps the interval is ±7.87 points and at 100 ±13.33. Gap accuracy needs fewer (about 160), because a gap is a smaller unit than a document's pages.
- **The rerun floor is on top of this.** The audit measured 1.4-3.2 points a box between identical reruns (SEG-10). The gate's interval covers sampling the held-out set; a model's own wobble at temperature 0 under vLLM is a second error of about the same size, so a 3-point difference is only separated when both are inside it: the gate should score two runs, or demand the difference clear the larger floor.
- **These are owner gaps, and the kit's user has tags.** The interval is sampling only; a gate scored against a user's tags inherits their noise (5.3% among agreed labels, `label-clean.md`), which shifts both models' scores alike in a paired difference but caps any absolute claim.

## The gate: `train_seg.py check` and the manifest

`train_seg.py check` (institutional-compute) is the kit's held-out gate. It reads `cluster_seg.py` answers only, scores each run's per-gap calls on the held-out collections against their tags (and against the owner's rulings when a file of them is given) with a folder bootstrap, and on a locked public anchor box (pages exact, gap accuracy, the review load to 98%) with a document bootstrap, each paired against the shipped model. It writes H §6.3's manifest: kit version, prompt id, base build, weights, adapter and training-data hashes, the scores with intervals, the Mac figures and the gate's verdict, and exits 1 when the gate fails. The proposed rule: an anchor score, a held-out score, and a held-out interval against the shipped model whose low end is above -3 points. Whether that is the acceptance rule is Phase 4's call (gap audit item 20).

The app reads the manifest as `archive-seg-manifest.json` beside the weights. `LocalSegmenter.refusal` runs the shipped build as its own, and refuses any other Model setting that is not a folder on the Mac holding a manifest of kit `seg-kit-1`, prompt c1, with an anchor pages-exact score: it is returned before a run starts (`WindowStageTransport.current`), as an unknown voter is refused today. Six checks in `SegmentationEngineTestDriver` cover it.

The shipped build's own manifest is `seg-kit-manifest-shipped.json`: weights `e4e00067fe02…` (the 8-bit MLX build), anchor Lincoln 34.87% of pages exact (23.26 to 49.28), gap accuracy 82.31%, review load to 98% 96.2 gaps per 100 pages. Its anchor answers are the full-precision FarmShare run, and its held-out run is not on this Mac, so its held-out block is empty and its own gate says so; the app requires only the anchor and the prompt.

## The kit end to end, dry (2026-10-31)

Each step run as far as it can go without a cluster (no engine session contacts Sherlock) and without training on this Mac (W46.kit-mac-lora measures that):

| step | how it was run | result |
|---|---|---|
| training set | `train_seg.py data`, offline test on a synthetic pack (`test_institutional_compute.Check`) | only whole agreed training windows train, held-out never; start-trust keeps more |
| train and merge | the README's `slurm_args` recipe against the example profile, printed not submitted | one sbatch line for `train.sbatch` (train, then merge) |
| held-out and anchor answers | `submit.sh seg seg-student --pack … --dry-run` (example profile) | one sbatch line serving the merged student under its name; the packs come from `corpus_pairs.py subpack --split holdout` and `public_segmentation.py pack` |
| the gate | `train_seg.py check` on the cached FarmShare runs (Lincoln; the joint 2B's held-out run) | reproduces the bench's figures (4B 46.7%, 2B 34.9% on Lincoln); joint 2B 80.4% of 7,277 held-out tag gaps |
| the app | `LocalSegmenter.refusal` on the written manifests | the shipped manifest passes; no manifest, no anchor score or another prompt is refused |

What the dry run found missing from the kit:

- **No step builds the Mac model.** The 8-bit MLX build was made by the Vision OCR lab's `convert-mlx.py` by hand (`seg-students.md` §On the Mac); the kit stops at a merged model on the cluster. A user needs a `train_seg.py` or kit step that converts and quantises it, then hashes what the app will load.
- **The manifest has to be copied beside the Mac build by hand**, and its weights hash is of whatever folder `--weights` named: hashed on the cluster's merged model, it does not match the Mac build.
- **Mac figures are not measured by the kit.** H §6.3 asks for the kit's own warm-up run; `check` takes them from a file (`--mac`), and nothing writes that file yet.
- **The local copy of the shipped build has no manifest** (`~/.local/share/visionocr-ocrlab/mlx/`): a Model setting naming that folder is now refused until `seg-kit-manifest-shipped.json` is copied there as `archive-seg-manifest.json`. The default setting (the published repository) is unaffected.
- The plan-route arm (a `claude -p` voter in the kit) is held for the gateway (CLAUDE.md, "Plan routes wait for the gateway"), so the coding-agent leg was not run through `claude -p`. The five-hour window went from 26% to 39% over this session, which shares it with anything else running; the kit's own steps were seconds of CPU.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The owner's ruled boxes (Dean, Deaver, Herrnstein; test boxes Stanton and RG165) and the audited public truths of Lincoln and CREST; dating: the owner's year tags. Stanton still carries the full-magazine rule the owner re-ruled on 2026-10-29 (SEG-7), so its figures will move when it is re-ruled.
- **Selection.** Whole boxes, chosen before these models existed; the dating 131 by whether the year is in the ABBYY text, which is how they were chosen in W41.date-model (ABBYY is not an input here).
- **Comparison.** Every model on the same windows and the same request (`method_local.request`, the images at 1,024 px, temperature 0, thinking off), each on one L40S under vLLM 0.31, read from `method_openrouter.py`'s caches; the untrained 4B was run on the development boxes only.
- **Noise.** The bootstrap's intervals above; the rerun floor (1.4-3.2 points a box) is not in them. Shared-error counts are single runs.
- **Scope.** The owner's material and two public boxes. The checkpack and Sasha arms, which measure accuracy on owner-ruled held-out gaps, wait for the Sherlock job (above).

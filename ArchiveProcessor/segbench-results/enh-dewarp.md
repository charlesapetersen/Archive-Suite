## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The owner's 96 pages (99 less the three agreement-only letters), truth drafted by Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash and adjudicated by the owner where they differed (`ocr-truth.md`), corrected on 2026-10-29 (W46.truth-edits). Words the two drafters wrote alike were not adjudicated; since then the owner has checked samples of them blind: 59 places on 20 pages where Qwen3.6-27B differs (answered 2026-10-21, `ocr-truth-bias-score.md`: 4 truth errors, the bias not material) and every place where Qwen3.6-27B or LightOnOCR-3 4B differs on the 16 hand pages, with 19 typed controls (answered 2026-10-31, `ocr-hands-rescore.md`: 12 corrections, 10 places unresolved). Agreed words that no third-family reader disputes stay unchecked, so a drafter-family route can still gain where the truth shares its mistake. Every table is scored on that truth as it stands when the report is generated.
- **Selection.** The 96 as they are, no page chosen by an arm's result. Every page where Apple Vision (26.6) read fewer than 20 words was replaced at the draw (19 of them, 4 tagged Handwritten; `ocr-truth.md`), so the 96 hold no page Vision reads as empty, and every comparison with Vision on them is on pages Vision could read. The tag groups are `capture-tags.json`'s defect tags, set without reading any arm. The hand crops are the six worst typed originals on the saved routes' scores (`L-capture.md` claim 43), so they are chosen by low raw readings, Vision's among them, and a crop's gain there is not a typical page's. The ladder pages are the truth set's resolution ladder (`ocr-truth.md`).
- **Comparison.** Each arm against raw, one reader at a time, the same reader settings and the same truth, paired by page. The Apple Vision arms are macOS 26.6 readings (2026-10-22) paired with the 26.6 reading of raw (`lines/raw-26.6`), not with the bench's 27.2 Vision route. Each Sherlock reader read raw again in the same run; Gemini's raw arm is W46.fix-ocr-scorers-paid's reading of the identical image. The arms were made from the images of 2026-10-22, so the 15 pages W46.truth-jpeg-upgrade later moved to their JPEG originals are their PDF images here, raw included.
- **Noise.** Paired 95% bootstrap intervals and permutation p on every row. The rerun floor on the 96 is `ocr-rerun-noise.md`'s: a gap under 0.49 NES points, or a paired interval across 0, is a tie. That floor is measured on Gemini and Qwen; Vision's own swing on a near-identical image (reading order, p008) can be tens of points on a page, and the tag groups are 10 to 52 pages.
- **Scope.** OCR input only: it does not judge the reading copy's look. The figures are 26.6 Vision's and do not carry to the 27.2 route until the arms are re-read on one OS; the 96 hold no page Vision reads as empty, so a dewarp that rescues an unreadable page cannot show here.

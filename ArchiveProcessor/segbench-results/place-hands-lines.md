# The shipped hand placer and its 0.67 gate, rescored on the line pass rate (W46.place-hands-lines)

Generated by `scripts/segbench/place_hands_lines.py` (tables between the markers; `place_hands_lines.py report`
rewrites only those). W46.place-hands-fu chose the hand route — blla lines, McCATMuS forced alignment, the per-page
`conf` matcher pick, a 0.67 CTC-confidence gate — on per-word Find, which the owner ruled is no evidence of
selection (c096de18; measurement audit OCR-11). Here every placement hypothesis `place_hands.py align` wrote (4
detectors × 13 placers and matcher picks = 52 routes, 4 readings) is written as a PDF text layer and every truth line
is dragged in PDFKit with W46.place-geom's scorer (`place_functional drag`). A line passes when the drag from its
first ink to its last returns exactly the reading's words for that line, in order, with no line break. The reading's
words for a line come from a word alignment of the reading to the truth. No model read a page here; the only model
work was kraken re-running the recognisers to align the new Qwen3.6-27B reading. Nothing left the Mac and no money
was spent.

## Verdict

- **On the owner's hand pages the shipped placer still wins on the line pass rate, with every reading.** With
  the app's line writer (the lab writer) on all 20 audited owner hand pages (475 scored lines): `blla.ctc-mccatmus-conf`
  is first or tied first. With the **non-drafter Qwen3.6-27B reading** it passes **42.9%** (204/475). The `cer` pick
  scores +0.4 (−0.5 to +1.5), a tie. Every other detector, recogniser or matcher is lower: line-count pick −2.1, RevCity
  −2.1 to −4.0, one-to-one −4.6, Orli −4.6 (−14.9 to +4.2), proportional placement −11.8, PP-OCRv6 lines −17.9,
  Surya −19.6, flow −40.2. The truth text gives the same order (44.0%), as do Gemini 3.8 Flash (41.3%, a drafter of
  this truth) and LightOnOCR-3 (21.5%, which reflows). **Keep the shipped default for the owner's material.**
- **On Bentham it does not win: PP-OCRv6's lines pass 16 to 18 points more.** On the 14 audited Bentham pages (314
  lines), PP-OCRv6 lines with the same CTC alignment pass 53.2% with Qwen3.6-27B (+17.5, +6.6 to +27.8) and 56.1%
  with the truth (+16.2, +5.5 to +26.5). The shipped placer passes 35.7% and 39.8%. PP-OCRv6 is ahead on 9 of 14 (2 level, 3 behind)
  pages. On Bentham the loss is blla's lines, not the aligner: blla with proportional placement is level with blla
  with CTC (−0.3 with the truth reading). On the owner pages PP-OCRv6's lines are 18 points worse. So the best
  detector depends on the collection. PP-OCRv6 is also not a Mac route at its pinned settings (one Bentham page
  peaked at 32 GB on CPU; place_hands.py's header). For the processing review's Phase 4 (W46.review-phase4): blla
  stays the default because the owner's material is what the app serves. The Bentham result is a reason to keep the
  detector a setting of the hand route, not hard-coded.
- **The 0.67 gate can only lower the line pass rate. What it buys is fewer failing drags on the image.** A gated
  page goes to the text page, so its lines are not selectable on the image at all. With Qwen3.6-27B's reading the gate
  sends 2 of 20 owner pages to the text page. That gives up 4 passing lines (−0.8 points, −2.8 to 0.0) and spares the
  user 39 failing drags, and the kept pages pass 46.3% instead of 42.9%. With the truth reading: 4 pages, 20 passing
  lines given up, 78 failing ones spared. With LightOnOCR-3's reflowed reading: 8 pages, 10 given up, 186 spared. No
  Bentham page falls under the gate with the truth or Qwen readings. The line score has no bar of its own to fit a
  threshold to. For Qwen, 0.67 sits on a flat stretch (0.57 to 0.70 keep the same 18 pages), and raising it to about
  0.75 lifts the kept pages to 52% at a cost of 19 passing lines. **Keep 0.67 as the starting value it was filed as.**
  Moving it trades passing lines for a cleaner layer, and that is a product call for Phase 4, not something this
  score settles.
- **Writer: the app's line writer beats one run per word on the owner's hands and loses on Bentham.** Shipped placer:
  +8.4 points (+1.2 to +15.1) with Qwen3.6-27B and +9.1 with the truth on the owner pages; −10.2 (−21.7 to +0.6) and
  −12.1 on Bentham. The truth's own lines through the line writer pass 85% of owner drags (404/475), and 34% one run
  per word. That is the scorer's ceiling on these hands, and the reason the per-word layer is no line-selection
  design here.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** Owner pages: the placement truth of `place_truth.py` (20 handwritten or mixed pages with audit verdict
  `ok`; lines and word boxes drawn by drawing subagents on the grid render and audited, the owner's line rulings for
  the hand pages folded in: place_geom HAND_MERGE, HAND_OPEN). Its text was drafted with Gemini 3.8 Flash among the
  routes, so the Gemini rows are a drafter's and are shown only beside the others. Bentham: the published PAGE-XML
  line geometry and transcription (line-only, no word boxes), which no route here drafted. **Qwen3.6-27B neither
  drafted nor checked either truth**: it is the non-drafter reading the audit asked for (saved readings, the same text
  `ocr_rescore.py` scores: the owner pages' from the cloud panel, Bentham's from the FarmShare 2026-10-19 open sweep;
  nothing was re-read).
- **Selection.** All audited hand pages of the placement truth (20 owner, 14 Bentham). None was chosen by a placer,
  detector, reading or ABBYY text. The item's "16 hand pages" predates the audit count.
- **Comparison.** Every route and reading is dragged along the same truth lines, the same way, with the same two
  writers. The reading's expected words for a line are the same for every placer, so a reader's own misses
  (`unread`) cost every route alike. Paired over pages.
- **Noise.** n = 20 and 14 pages; 95% intervals from a paired page bootstrap (2,000 draws, seed 46). Differences
  under about 2 points on the owner pages and about 10 on Bentham are inside them and are ties (the `cer` pick, Orli,
  the line-count pick on most readings). The drag scorer is deterministic (PDFKit, fixed layers). A placer rerun
  floor was not measured: kraken on CPU is deterministic in its code path, and the hypotheses are the saved ones.
- **Scope.** Does not settle: the writer the app's hand route should use, beyond these two (a per-line run through
  the CTC word positions was not built); Real5 or any other hand collection; a threshold other than 0.67 (above);
  PP-OCRv6 lines on the Mac (not runnable at its pinned settings); whether an off-image text page is better for the
  user than a failing drag. The drag runs along the truth line's baseline, lifted to mid-band, because Bentham has no
  word boxes. A line of fewer than two truth words, or more than 45° off level, is not scored.

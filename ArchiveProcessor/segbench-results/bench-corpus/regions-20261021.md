# W46.ocr-regions — read whole pages except where the small newspaper readers need regions

Measured from the collected 2026-10-21 pack, completed on Sherlock on 2026-10-23 and collected by the owner on
2026-10-24. Scored on the Mac on 2026-10-25. No new inference, paid calls, cluster contact or corpus writes in
this scoring session; $0 for this item. These are experimental routes, not a change to the app's defaults.

## What this changes

- **Use layout for the two small newspaper readers, not as a general pre-step.** On the 15 L9 scans,
  DocLayout-YOLO takes GLM-OCR from 29.2 to 91.1 NES and PaddleOCR-VL 1.5 from 0.0 to 92.3. PP-DocLayoutV3
  reaches 86.2 and 89.7. Whole LightOnOCR-3 4B and Qwen3.6-27B already reach 92.5 and 93.5; cropping loses
  4–11 points. The earlier GLM newspaper result near 97 used a different run/image setting: this result is 91.1,
  and is not substituted for that measurement.
- **Overlapping tiles do not reproduce the newspaper gain.** They reach only 8–35 NES on L9. Reading bands
  across columns and stitching them in geometric column/band order leaves the layout problem unsolved. This
  rules out this fixed tiling control as an adequate substitute; it does not rule out every possible tiler.
- **Keep whole-page reading on ordinary typed pages and hands.** On L3, detector crops lose 4–31 points;
  tiles are near whole for LightOn but slightly worse for the others. On L1, YOLO scores 3–4, PP regions
  68–78 versus 70–88 whole. YOLO discards 67 of 80 L1 pages as containing no kept text region. This confirms
  the hazard of applying that detector's print-oriented picture filter to handwriting; W46.layout-photo
  measures the detector alternatives. Small changes on the 96's 13 clippings or 16 handwritten pages are
  exploratory, not evidence of a universal kind router.
- **The owner's 96 favour whole pages.** Overall whole/PP/tiles: GLM 90.9/84.9/87.8; Paddle
  83.8/83.0/84.2; LightOn 89.4/80.5/84.0; Qwen 95.0/85.7/89.8. YOLO is 67.5–71.7.
  Typed pages without a table/clipping are the exception worth keeping visible: GLM tiles gain 2.1 and
  Paddle tiles 5.9 points on 41 pages, while LightOn and Qwen stay near whole. Those gains shrink substantially or reverse when
  pooling the owner's other page kinds, and the kind gate has not been trained or held-out tested here.
- **L4 remains hard.** Whole GLM/Paddle/LightOn/Qwen score 59.7/28.2/55.0/69.3; tiles reach
  57.9/35.8/52.9/72.6. PP regions improve Paddle to 37.2 but hurt the others; YOLO drops 33 of the 80
  register pages completely and retains little useful text on many others. Table-region-only and per-cell
  readings were not in the collected pack: W46.ocr-regions-cells carries that remaining experiment, including
  region/cell truth and ordering. These page-level numbers do not answer the cell-reading question.

## Frozen inputs and scoring

255 public pages: L9 newspaper scans 15; L4 Smithsonian Freedmen's Bureau registers 80; L3 typescripts 80;
L1 historical hands 80. All 99 original owner inputs were read; the same three agreement-only letters as the
panel are excluded from accuracy, leaving 96. Exact chart kinds are 50 typewritten, 28 printed, 16 handwritten
and 2 mixed (the overall chart includes mixed; the three kind charts do not). Annotation groups are separate,
may overlap, and are 13 clippings, 12 tables, 16 handwritten and 41 typewritten without a table/clipping.
The latter is an annotation-based proxy, not a measured single-column label. No 895-page pooled score is
claimed from these four public strata. The 96's original frozen images are used, including the PDF-image
limitation separately tracked in W46.fix-truth96-pdf-images; no replacement images enter this comparison.

Whole pages reuse each route's original cached readings, including its caps. Native prompts are GLM's own,
Paddle's own and the image alone for LightOn; native Markdown/HTML is scored as written. Qwen uses the panel
prompt and its FP8 weights with auto KV cache, including the owner whole run qwen3.6-27b-1849744 (94.97 NES).
That is different from the fp8-KV whole route on the main chart. All baseline image hashes that are recorded
match the region pack; Qwen's owner whole replies have no image hash, so their provenance rests on the
collected copy described in the original checkpoint notes (whole96-qwen3.6-27b-1849744/replies).

Crop settings: 2,400-pixel page copies, each route's same prompt, no thinking, temperature 0, 4,096 tokens per
crop (whole pages had 8,192), JPEG quality 95. Detector crops pad by 0.4% of page dimensions and widen tiny
sides to 32 pixels. PP-DocLayoutV3 boxes were detected on the original Mac images at 1,024 pixels; YOLO used
newspaper-ocr's detector and layout processing on the cluster copies. Each uses its own ordering; nested boxes
and picture labels are excluded. Tiles are bands of height 0.6 × column width, at least 15% overlap, with
columns chosen from page width / 2,000. Regions join as paragraphs; adjacent same-column tile text uses the
checkpointed word-alignment deduplicator. Crop/encoding/token budget/order all belong to the staged route;
this is not an isolated causal test of detection quality. The tiler/stitcher was adjusted on the three-page
pilot before the full scored collection; these are exploratory comparisons, not held-out tuning estimates.

Every reader has all 5,487 receipts (1,482 YOLO, 3,075 PP, 930 tiles) over 354 inputs. Caps are retained:
GLM 28, Paddle 169, LightOn 14, Qwen 41; zero errors. A cap contributes no text; surviving crops may produce
a partial page, counted as answered and scored against the entire page's truth. All-capped or empty-detection
pages score zero. YOLO has no kept regions on 116 inputs; PP on 8. Positive-count missing crops/pages,
mismatched identities/geometry or absent token usage now fail export rather than shrinking the denominator.
The 38 successful GLM FarmShare pilot crops were reused; other crop results came from Sherlock.

NES is the existing ocr_panel_score scorer, with public-stratum normalization unchanged; it is a page mean,
not a word-weighted mean. Paired changes use whole on exactly the same pages, including failures. chrF and
word counts are retained in the CSV. Reading-order edit is over matched truth blocks only: each arm's matched
page count is shown; the means use different page subsets and are not paired evidence of an order improvement.
The 96's truth is kept in one line, so reading order is unscorable there. Small subsets and many comparisons
limit rankings based on small differences.

## Strict results

NES % (pages answered / pages); a failed page scores 0. Δ is the arm against whole page on the same pages, in points.
Order: mean reading-order edit (matched-page count) over pages with two or more matched truth blocks;
lower is better, but each arm has its own matched-page subset, so these means are not paired.
not scorable on the 96, whose truth is kept as one line with no paragraph breaks.

## Cost, timing and remaining arms

Active reading spans, one L40S per job, are 0.09/0.07/0.12/0.59 GPU-hours for GLM/Paddle/LightOn/Qwen.
These exclude model startup and combine all three arms and both input sets; do not infer per-arm throughput,
photos an hour, or a matched whole-versus-crop speedup. The separate earlier newspaper speed result is not
remeasured by these summaries. No real money was spent by this item.

The bench includes all twelve crop routes on the overall and exact-kind OCR charts. For Qwen, a hypothetical
OpenRouter charge uses the 96 pages' own crop token totals, including caps, at the committed historical list
rate ($0.25/$2 per million input/output tokens, read 2026-10-20); it is not a measured Sherlock bill or a new
hosted measurement. The other three have no hosted price in the checked catalogue and are marked free on a
university cluster. No per-arm throughput is fabricated. Part 5's difficulty/cascade analysis excludes these
routes until they are in ocr-pages.csv. The public comparison is only these four strata.

W46.ocr-regions-paid retains the hosted Gemini arm and the held Claude arm, routed through the gateway,
never the plan. W46.ocr-regions-cells retains table-region-only and per-cell readings on L4 with suitable
region/cell truth and the forms benchmark's split. Neither arm is claimed measured here.

## Reproduction

Use a venv with scripts/segbench/ocr-panel-requirements.txt and python-Levenshtein. The private cache and
Long term storage copies are inputs only; export to a new scratch directory, then copy the score-only files:

```sh
<venv>/bin/python ArchiveProcessor/scripts/segbench/corpus/regions.py report \
  --runs ~/Library/Caches/ArchiveSuiteRehearsal/sherlock-results/regions-20261021 \
  --out /tmp/regions-report
python3 ArchiveOCRBench/build.py
python3 ArchiveOCRBench/build.py --check
```

The four generated .json/.csv/-pages.csv/-tables.md files are committed beside this report; no page text or
pixels are committed. The JSON includes the independent exact-kind groups and the table-markup diagnostic.
Offline geometry, failure, coverage, usage and stitching regressions: corpus/test_regions.py. Bench regression:
ArchiveOCRBench/test_regions.py. The published copy of ArchiveOCRBench is behind until an interactive publish.

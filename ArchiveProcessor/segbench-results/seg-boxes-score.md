## Reading

On held-out Sasha the shipped Mac 2B scores 89.7% on 175 known gaps (pages exact 37.4%), against the teacher's 67.4% and its bf16 source's 89.1%. The source's pages exact is 46.6%: similar gap accuracy can leave different long documents wrong, and these single runs do not rank quantizations. The Illustrated Review and SC0122 are trained-on diagnostics for the students, not evidence of generalization. SC0122's supported comparison is its owner-ruled disagreement gaps plus controls weighted to the agreed strata; document-exact scores there remain conditional on tag/teacher-drafted truth. Gemini 3.7 Flash scores 96.0% on held-out Sasha (pages exact 82.2%); paired against the Mac 2B it is 6.3 points ahead on gap accuracy (sign test p 0.019), and far ahead on whole documents. On the Illustrated Review it merges 40 article gaps, because g1 still asks for the full magazine issue (W46.seg-magazine-rule-redo), so its pages exact there is no test of reading ability. Across three cloud offsets, no-check exact pages range 83.0%–85.2% and checks per 100 pages 25.9–27.3; the 3.8-fitted flag level is unchanged.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The three locked boxes' truths (Sasha owner-ruled from label tags, locked 2026-10-30; the Illustrated Review owner-ruled on the article-within-issue rule; SC0122 tags and teacher where they agreed, the owner's rulings where they did not, locked 2026-10-29/30) with every unsure gap out; Stanton and RG165 as options.md scores them (Stanton with the owner's 2026-10-31 sheet rulings). No scored route drafted a truth, except that SC0122's agreed stratum is where the teacher agreed with the tag: the owner's 60 blind controls on it found no error, and the SC0122 section reports it apart.
- **Selection.** The boxes whole, named by the owner before any of these routes ran on them; the offset boxes are options.md's test set. Nothing chosen by a tested route or by ABBYY text.
- **Comparison.** Every route on the same windows and Vision text, scored on the same known gaps and resolved documents. The Mac 2B and the Sherlock models see 1,024 px images and prompt l1; Gemini sees 1,600 px and g1, as each is run in the bench and the app. Both OCR sets do not apply: this is segmentation.
- **Noise.** Single runs at temperature 0. The cloud route's window offsets move no-check exact pages by 2.1 points; a difference between routes smaller than the offset spread, or than 1.4-3.2 points a box, is a tie. Sign tests are sampling only.
- **Scope.** Three owner boxes and two test boxes. It does not settle a prompt change for the article-within-issue rule (g1 still says full magazine: W46.seg-magazine-rule-redo), 3.8 against 3.7 on these boxes, or a level fitted for 3.7.

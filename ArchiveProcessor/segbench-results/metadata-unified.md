## What it says

- **One text-only call holds the correspondence fields and the date.** Sender and recipient tie with the dedicated entity call (intervals over zero); the sending place loses only its normalised form, which a format rule in the prompt supplies. The year ties the tagging call's and every neighbour-sourced year carries Date Uncertain, with a quote that passes the dating route's evidence check on most documents.
- **It does not hold the printed pieces' title, byline and publication date against the image-reading item-field call**, which sees the masthead and the margin that Vision's text often lacks. A unified call that replaces the item-field call has to carry the page images too (the item-field call already sends them at 1,600 px, about 1,100 image tokens a document).
- **Enclosure proposals from text alone are conservative and quoted**: a few documents in ten announce one, about one in seventy is proposed as sent with the document before it, and the quotes are in the text.
- **Cost and time fall to about a third and a fifth.** One call's prompt is longer than any of the four, but the four repeat the document's opening three times and the entity call's default thinking alone costs more than the whole unified reply at thinking low.
- **Open:** the lead cloud models (the gateway, 1 December), the open model (the Sherlock 27B pack), and an image-carrying arm. Filed as W49.proc-metadata-consolidate-lead; the implementation behind it as W49.proc-metadata-unified-apply.

## The image pass under a VLM reader

The dating route's image pass (prompts.md §9, 2,400 px) is sent where the document's own text has no year candidate, which under Apple Vision is 32% of the owner's mix (date-route-pipeline.md). Whether it still adds anything when Stage 04 reads with a VLM is not measured here: the cache holds no VLM OCR text for the documents whose year is on the page but not in the text (W46.date-image's 122). What the cache does say: on those 122, the image pass found the document's own date on 56 pages (date-image.md), of which 23 were typed, 18 printed, 10 handwritten and 5 stamped. A VLM reader transcribes typed and printed dates into the text as a matter of course, so the pass's remaining yield under a VLM reader is bounded by the handwritten and stamped dates, 15 of the 56 (about 12% of the 122, or 4% of the owner's mix), plus whatever typed dates the VLM itself misses. The direct test, filed with W49.proc-metadata-consolidate-lead: the gateway's Gemini OCR of those 122 documents' first two pages (about $0.40), counting the owner's years the VLM text carries that Vision's lacks, against the pass's 56.

- **Comparison.** The same Vision text (the first 8 and last 2 pages) and the same neighbours for every arm of the unified call. - **Scope.** One text route (Apple Vision), one set, one Gemini model at one thinking setting; the dating trace was not run on this set, so the date comparison is against the tagging call's year, the app's year before the dating call.

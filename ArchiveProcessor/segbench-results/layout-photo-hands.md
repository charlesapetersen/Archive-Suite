# W46.layout-photo — the four detectors on handwriting, before any reader

Generated by `scripts/segbench/layout_photo.py hands`; per-page figures in `layout-photo-hands.json`. Ink: pixel < 128 at 1000 px long side, 2% margin excluded, a proxy for text. "Read" is what the region step would send to a reader (`regions_cluster.keep_regions`: no picture labels, no box 90% inside a larger kept one).

- **yolo**: DocLayout-YOLO DocStructBench ONNX, imgsz 1024, conf 0.25, app region sort; median 1.77 s a page on this Mac
- **pp**: PP-DocLayoutV3 (mlx-vlm port), conf 0.5, 1024 px, model reading order; median 0.27 s a page on this Mac
- **heron**: docling-layout-heron (RT-DETRv2), 640 x 640, conf 0.3, app region sort; median 0.19 s a page on this Mac
- **vision**: Apple Vision RecognizeDocumentsRequest (macOS 26): title, paragraphs, tables, lists; its order; median 0.43 s a page on this Mac

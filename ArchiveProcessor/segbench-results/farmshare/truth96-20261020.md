# The 895-page open sweep's models on the owner's 96 truth pages (FarmShare, 20 Oct 2026)

**A review baseline, nothing adopted.** This is the 96-page half of "Both OCR sets, every time" (root `CLAUDE.md`)
for the models Phase 0 of the processing review scored on the 895 public pages
([`bench-corpus/open-sweep-20261019.md`](../bench-corpus/open-sweep-20261019.md)). No default, catalogue entry or
recommendation changes because of it. Free cluster time; no hosted model was called and no money was spent.

Protocol and paths: [`execution-plans/truth96-farmshare-20261020.md`](../../../execution-plans/truth96-farmshare-20261020.md).
Seven of the eight jobs finished on 20 Oct between 21:37 and 22:05 PDT. Ling 3 Flash VL (job 1861703, four L40S on
one node) is still waiting in the queue and is not in this report.

Scorer: `scripts/segbench/corpus/farmshare_truth96.py`. It reads each collector reading with
`farmshare_expanded_report.reading()` in the same mode as on the 895 (panel, native or pipeline) and scores it with
`ocr_panel_score.score()` against the panel's truth texts, as `cluster_ocr.py truth` did for the earlier FarmShare
truth runs. Caps, errors, blanks and missing pages score 0. The three agreement-only letters (p086, p094, p096) are
left out of the 96 and reported apart. Per-model results are in `truth/<model>-<job>/summary.json` and
`pages.csv` (page scores and strata, no text). The raw readings are outside git, in
`~/Claude/Long term storage/FarmShare-truth96-20261020/outputs/`.

```
cd ArchiveProcessor/scripts/segbench/corpus
O=".../FarmShare-truth96-20261020/outputs"
uv run --offline --with rapidfuzz --with 'sacrebleu==2.5.1' --with regex --with numpy python farmshare_truth96.py \
  --out ../../../segbench-results/farmshare/truth --sacct "$O/sacct.txt" \
  --route "glm-ocr=$O/glm-ocr:native" --route "lightonocr3-0.8b=$O/lightonocr3-08b:native" \
  --route "lightonocr3-4b=$O/lightonocr3-4b:native" --route "paddleocr-vl1.5=$O/paddleocr-vl15:native" \
  --route "qwen3.5-4b=$O/qwen35-4b:panel" --route "qwen3-vl-8b=$O/qwen3-vl-8b:panel" \
  --route "ppocrv6-medium=$O/ppocrv6-medium-native:pipeline"
```

## Settings

The settings are the 895-page runs'. Each runner refused to start unless its cache contract matched the sweep's
in every field. That means the same revisions, routes, prompts and collector, vLLM 0.31.0 on one L40S, bf16
weights, context 16,384, bf16 KV cache, 0.90 memory, 16 sequences, eight requests at a time, temperature 0,
thinking off and 8,192 output tokens. PP-OCRv6 is the same stock medium detector and recogniser pipeline
(PaddleOCR 3.7.0), checked against the 895 run's `contract.json`. Every page went in as the frozen 2400 px JPEG 90
the panel sent. Every reading's image SHA-256 matches the panel manifest's original for all 99 pages and all seven
models, and the manifest matches the cached image bytes.

material (Kurrent, charters, Greek, Hebrew, Arabic, Chinese), where the small panel-prompt Qwens fail many pages.
The 96 are mostly English typescript and print from 1900 on.

## Failures

No reading is missing, errored, blank or unparsable. Every failure is a reply that ran to the 8,192-token cap and
was scored as empty. All but one cap is repetitive (zlib ratio below 0.12, the 895 report's threshold).

- Qwen3-VL 8B: p018. Qwen3.5 4B: p025. GLM-OCR: p100. LightOnOCR-3 0.8B: p094, an agreement-only letter, so it
  answers all of the 96. Each of these is a loop.
- PaddleOCR-VL 1.5: 11 caps, 10 of them loops: p013, p018, p025, p048, p050, p054, p081, p082, p088, p093 and p094.
  Its NES on the pages it answered (93.53) is as high as any of the seven, so its 83.78 is mostly the caps. The same
  pattern is in its 895 run (545/895 answered).
- LightOnOCR-3 4B and PP-OCRv6 answered all 99.

## The call

Fixed before any answer: offer the tier if, at the frozen level, pages right after checking fall by no more than the 3.2-point rerun floor, unflagged errors stay within two of the control's, and it costs at most 60% of the image arm. Measured: after checking -1.5 points, unflagged errors +1, cost 48%. **A budget tier is worth offering.**

## Reading

- **What the tier saves is money; what it costs is checking.** At the shipped level text alone asks the user to check 38.3 boundaries per 100 pages against 25.3 with images, to save $1.88 per 1,000 pages at list. The call fixed beforehand did not weigh checks; a tier offered on it should show that figure beside the price.
- **Its one unflagged error is a gap every cheap route misses.** Text alone accepts Stanton 144|145 as a continuation; Claude only and GLM-5.3 Flash only leave the same gap unflagged with images (options.md).
- **The level is the image arm's.** Text alone is less sure of the same decisions, so a level fitted on its own development answers would differ (fewer checks, or more errors). Shipping the tier would fit one first, as options.py fits every approach's.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The owner's ruled test boxes, Stanton (with the owner's 2026-10-31 newsletter rulings on the article rule) and RG165, truth photo labels; no unsure gaps, and no route drafted any of it.
- **Selection.** The two locked test boxes options.py scores on, whole, fixed since 2026-10-11; the level was frozen on the development boxes before this run; no ABBYY text.
- **Comparison.** One model (Gemini 3.7 Flash), one route, prompt g2, the same windows, Vision text, settings and scorer; the text arm drops only the images and the one paragraph about them. The level is the image arm's, not refit for text alone.
- **Noise.** One run per arm, 363 pages. Paired bootstrap intervals over truth documents cover document sampling, not rerun variation; the rerun floor is 1.4-3.2 points of pages exact a box (SEG-10), so a gap inside it is a tie. The error counts are a few gaps, so a difference of one or two is within noise.
- **Scope.** Gemini 3.7, not the 3.8 the app sends (the gateway does not serve 3.8). Two boxes of one archive; not a level fitted for text alone, and not other material. Whether the app offers the tier is a product call on these figures.

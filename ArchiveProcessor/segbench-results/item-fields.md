## Limits

- The 60 articles and reports were drawn by Gemini's own `format`, so Gemini's kind on them is favoured; the 159 W46 documents were drawn by Gemini's format too (letters, memos, telegrams) or by text cues.
- The 72 W46 additions have no cached app replies; the app is scored on the documents that have them. Their fresh Gemini replies are a deferred paid run (execution-plans/paid-runs-deferred.md).
- Readers saw the first page and the first 3,000 characters only: titles, bylines and mastheads sit there, but a recipient address or a running head on a later page is missed.
- The app's date is its estimate of the document's date, not a printed issue date; on a printed piece with no printed date it counts as filled where the truth is empty. Recall is the share right where a date is printed.
- Model-drafted and model-adjudicated by two open 8-12B models (STATE Phase 2 decision 2), no owner pass; the pages were not read again by the session that built it (corpus pages are read as data by the gateway or an open model, never a Claude session; owner, 2026-10-30). It checked the scorer instead, by reading the predicted and true values of mismatches: some truth publications are misread (a column name or a staff title for the masthead), so the publication row is a floor, not an exact figure.

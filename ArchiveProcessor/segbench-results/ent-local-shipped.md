## Measurement card

- **Truth**: W46.fix-ent-truth's 138 correspondence documents (model truth: GPT 5.6 Luna and Claude Sonnet 5 drafts, Sonnet rulings; no Gemini or Qwen drafted or ruled), with the owner's answers on the fields his two sheets asked and the written-place rule (ent-truth-owner.md); scored also against the truth as built.
- **Selection**: as W46.ent-score-paid: 82 correspondence documents of W41's date-tagging sample plus 56 chosen by ABBYY text cues, so 56 of the 138 were chosen by the ABBYY text (owner, 2026-10-30); none by a route tested here. The ABBYY-reply rows exist for the 82 only, so the like-for-like rows are "on the 82".
- **Comparison**: one prompt, built by one function, for every Vision arm, with the same text and context; temperature 0; the local models capped at 2048 tokens (finish counts above) and Gemini at 8,192. The ABBYY rows are the old prompt on the ABBYY text layer, shown as the rows these replace, not as a like-for-like arm: prompt and text both differ.
- **Noise**: n as given; paired bootstrap intervals over the documents both arms answered. The Gemini rerun floor is ent-prompt-app.md's; the local models' rerun floor is not measured (greedy decoding at temperature 0). A gap whose interval spans zero is a tie.
- **Scope**: correspondence only; text only (the 8B's own reading of the page image is ent-robust.md's route); one Mac (18 GB), so a 27B on a 32 GB Mac stays unmeasured; the 27B's cluster seconds are throughput under concurrency, not a per-document time on any Mac.

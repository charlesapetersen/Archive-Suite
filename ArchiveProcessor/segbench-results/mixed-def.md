Generated by `scripts/segbench/mixed_def.py` on 2026-10-22. The 2000 pages are the FarmShare bulk pilot's random draw from 91,152 corpus PDFs in 33 collections (`farmshare/bulk/`, neutral page names). No money: Apple Vision, open weights, and labels already saved. Per-page labels and the images stay in the private cache.

## The owner's sheet

170 pages, about 57 minutes: 100 disagree, 39 mixed, 20 control, 11 mixed-both (the disagreements spread over 29 patterns; mixed-both tops up the 39 all-mixed pages with pages ATRIUM and Qwen both call mixed while another label dissents, and counts for the cut but not the corpus share; the controls are pages every label calls typed or printed, so the score can check the agreed pages rather than assume them clean). Blind: each page asks how much handwriting it carries and whether the owner would call it mixed; no label is shown. Seed 4608.

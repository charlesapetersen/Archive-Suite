## Checked by image

A random 100 pairs a method (all of them where fewer). The PDF page is rendered and compared with its JPEG by a 16x16 difference hash at the JPEG's four quarter turns; the pair matches when its JPEG is nearer the page than each of the 4 JPEGs nearest it in its folder, the most alike wrong answers there are. A tie is settled when one candidate is nearer than the rest by 8 bits.

## Training windows

From the 117 per-document folders that remain once 3 corpus copies of the development and test boxes are set aside (folder names matching Stanton, RG 165, Deaver, Dean or Herrnstein; that also takes Harvard's Office of the Dean papers, kept out because which corpus folder the Dean box came from is not recorded). Seven-page windows at stride 3 over runs of consecutive paired pages; a page with no JPEG breaks the run. Labels by corpus_survey's tag rule. 22 of 114 collections are held out whole (about 15% of implied boundaries). 30% of training windows go with no page text, because in the app the segmenter sees the app's own OCR, never ABBYY's; the rest carry ABBYY's text layer. App-style OCR text for a share of windows would need OCR of the pages, which this item does not do.

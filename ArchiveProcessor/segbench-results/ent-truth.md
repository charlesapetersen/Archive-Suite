## Limits

- Handwriting is thin: 5 handwritten letters. The agreed pool needs ABBYY text, and most handwritten pages have little or none, so the garbled-text cue found few and most of what it found were not letters. A handwritten set would have to come from the JPEG mirror's Handwritten-tagged pages, which have no ABBYY text to score text extractors on.
- The no-dateline cue confirmed none of its 18 (a date line the OCR mangled is still a date line to a reader of the image); the no-dateline count above is what the readers found across the whole set.
- 1 document left out: the gateway's content filter refused reader B on it three times (a Stanford admissions memo; nothing in it is sensitive). One other document's B draft came back empty twice at low effort and was drafted at default reasoning.
- Model-drafted and model-adjudicated (STATE Phase 2 decision 2); no owner pass. The session that built it spot-checked a sample against the page images (W46.fix-ent-truth's DONE entry).
- Only the first and last page images were shown; a name only legible on a middle page is missed.
- The additions come from the agreed, year-tagged pool with ABBYY text, as the 300 did: undated documents and documents without text are not in it.
- The cached tagging replies W46.ent-score reads cover the 88 only; the additions need fresh replies.

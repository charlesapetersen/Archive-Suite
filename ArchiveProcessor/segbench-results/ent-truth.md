## Limits

- Handwriting is thin: 5 handwritten letters. The agreed pool needs ABBYY text, and most handwritten pages have little or none, so the garbled-text cue found few and most of what it found were not letters. A handwritten set would have to come from the JPEG mirror's Handwritten-tagged pages, which have no ABBYY text to score text extractors on.
- The no-dateline cue confirmed none of its 18 (a date line the OCR mangled is still a date line to a reader of the image); the no-dateline count above is what the readers found across the whole set.
- 1 document left out: the gateway's content filter refused reader B on it three times (a Stanford admissions memo; nothing in it is sensitive). One other document's B draft came back empty twice at low effort and was drafted at default reasoning.
- Model-drafted and model-adjudicated (STATE Phase 2 decision 2); no owner pass. The session that built it spot-checked a sample against the page images (W46.fix-ent-truth's DONE entry).
- Only the first and last page images were shown; a name only legible on a middle page is missed.
- The additions come from the agreed, year-tagged pool with ABBYY text, as the 300 did: undated documents and documents without text are not in it.
- The cached tagging replies W46.ent-score reads cover the 88 only; the additions need fresh replies.

## Measurement card

The full card for the truth as it now stands, with the owner's answers and the place rule, is `ent-truth-owner.md`'s; this one is for the truth as built.

- **Truth.** Model truth: GPT 5.6 Luna and Claude Sonnet 5 drafts, Sonnet adjudicating, no owner pass when built. The readers' agreement with it (above) is the scorer's self-check, not their accuracy. Since then the owner answered 23 fields on 20 documents, all of them Sonnet's rulings for its own reader (W46.ent-truth-owner-fold). Sonnet ruled for its own reader on sender 17, recipient 18 and sending place 33 fields of the 138; the owner's sheet asked 5, 6 and 12, so 12, 12 and 21 self-rulings were never asked (the place rule has since emptied 14 of those places; 8 senders and 4 recipients stay filled where the readers named different people). The owner overturned 3 of the 7 asked sender and recipient rulings he settled (Wilson about 0.16 to 0.75), which suggests about 2-3% of those fields are wrong in the truth: small beside the gaps the entity reports give, but a truth error the scores do not carry (counts from measurement review pass 11, P11-5).
- **Selection.** The date-tagging sample's letters and 56 additions drawn by cue strata from the agreed, year-tagged pool with ABBYY text, so chosen by ABBYY text (undated documents and documents without text are not in it); no route scored against it chose a document.
- **Comparison.** Both readers drafted blind from the same first and last page images and the same first 8,000 characters of text, with the same prompt.
- **Noise.** One draft per reader and one ruling per dispute; no rerun of either, so how often a redraw would change a field is not measured.
- **Scope.** Correspondence documents only, typed-heavy (5 handwritten letters), dated ones only; a name legible only on a middle page is missed.

# The blind handwriting sheet (W46.ocr-hands-sheet)

Generated by `scripts/segbench/ocr_hands_sheet.py` from saved readings only: no call, no money, and no Claude reader looked at the pages. It answers the measurement audit's OCR-1 and OCR-4 (`execution-plans/processing-review/11-measurement-audit.md`): the 96's truth was drafted by Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 3.8 leads on the hand pages, and the earlier blind check (`ocr-truth-bias.md`) drew about 3 hand pages and looked only where Qwen3.6-27B differed.

## What is on it

- **Hand places.** The 16 hand pages of the 96 (kind handwritten, less the three agreement pages). Every place where Qwen3.6-27B or LightOnOCR-3 4B reads the truth differently, agreed and adjudicated words alike: 137 places, the two readers' overlapping differences merged. Left out: 15 that differ only in where the words break, and 8 longer than 6 words on a side (a passage left out, added or moved: reading order, not reading). **114 places on 16 pages are on the sheet**; 35 show three readings (the truth and both readers all differ), 87 lie wholly in agreed words and 27 touch a word the owner adjudicated. Places where each reader differs: qwen3.6-27b 85, lightonocr3-4b 83.
- **Typed controls.** 20 passages of 8 words drawn at random (seed 20261030) from the 21,345 eligible starts on 77 typed and printed pages (p081 left out: the owner cannot correct it): agreed words that both readers also read word for word, the part no check has looked at. They fall on 17 pages, mixed among the hand pages. Each shows its one reading, shared by every reader and both drafters, and asks whether it is right.

Blind: a place shows its readings unlabelled in a random order, and nothing on the sheet says which is the truth; that is kept in a key the sheet never loads. Each place carries a rough box: 118 from the placement truth's word boxes (`place_truth.py`), 16 from Apple Vision's word positions, and 0 with none, which say how far through the page's text they are. A box comes from the truth's word positions, shared by every reading of the place, so it does not tell them apart.

33 pages, about 27 minutes at `ocr_truth.py`'s review rates (an estimate). The sheet and its key are private, under `ocr-truth/hands/`. W46.ocr-hands-rescore folds the answers (`ocr_hands_sheet.py fold`), corrects the hand truth, rescores the hand charts and C-ocr's hand route, and reports the controls' error rate.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The 96's truth as it stands: drafted by Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash, the drafters' disagreements adjudicated by the owner. This sheet is the check of it on the hand pages; nothing is scored yet.
- **Selection.** Places are every difference of two third-family readers, not a sample; the controls are a seeded random draw. Neither is chosen by a drafter, and no ABBYY text is read.
- **Comparison.** None yet: the rescore compares routes on the corrected truth.
- **Noise.** 114 hand places and 20 controls; the rescore gives intervals.
- **Scope.** Places where both third-family readers agree with a wrong truth are not on the sheet; the controls estimate how often that happens on typed pages only, and on hand pages it stays unmeasured. Passages longer than the block limit (reading order) are not judged.

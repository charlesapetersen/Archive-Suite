## What it shows

The drafter-family routes lead qwen3.6-27b on the agreed words by +2.23 points on average, and by +0.85 once a line-end hyphen kept or joined is forgiven: most of the strict lead is the drafters' convention of keeping the hyphen, which the S0 score does not join. What remains is the most the bias could explain. qwen3.6-27b misses 1.34% of the agreed words with joins forgiven; if every one of those were the truth's mistake rather than the reader's, its score on the agreed words would rise by that much, and if none were, there is no bias. The adjudicated words do not settle it: they are the hard places where the drafters disagreed, so every route scores far lower there, and with 611 words the intervals are wide. The blind pass below measured the share directly.

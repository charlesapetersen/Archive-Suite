## Conclusion (scored 2026-10-28: the Mac runs and the Sherlock collection of 2026-10-24)

- **Log-probabilities do rank a reader's own errors, once its caps are set aside.** On answered pages, mean token log-probability (or min-k, or the low-probability share, all within a few hundredths of each other) has AUC 0.76-0.94 against a reading under 90 NES, for both readers, on the Mac and on Sherlock. That is about as good as two-reader disagreement (0.74-0.98) for the price of one reading. The first token and output length do not rank them.
- **They miss the worst failure.** A reading that loops to the cap is confident: against failure on the public sets, mean log-probability has AUC 0.04-0.26, the wrong way round. Any use has to escalate caps and errors first (fail+mean); length and disagreement catch loops directly.
- **Calibration does not travel across a stratum mix.** Isotonic on the 440 (the original strata, far more errors) leaves Qwen3.5-9B's mean ECE at 0.38 on the 455 and 0.71 on the 96, and the escalated pages' calibrated P overstates their error rate (0.78 against 0.40): UCCI's invariance assumption fails. With caps first it mostly holds: fail+mean's ECE is 0.05 on the 455 and 0.04 on the 96 for Qwen (escalated pages 0.64 calibrated against 0.62 observed), 0.11 and 0.02 for LightOnOCR.
- **But it does not choose the pages worth escalating.** Low confidence marks pages that are hard for every reader: AUC about 0.88 against Qwen3.6-27B also reading under 90. Escalating the least confident fifth of answered pages gains less than escalating the rest (LightOnOCR: -2.0 NES points there against +3.2; Qwen3.5-9B: +0.4 against +1.6). In the 20%-budget cascade on the 455, fail+mean (72.48, 73.53) does no better than escalating caps and errors alone (72.63, 73.72), against 77.34 and 75.53 for hindsight. The threshold chosen on the 96 escalates every page for most signals, because the 27B reads nearly every one of the 96 a little better.
- **For the app:** no change to W41.app-ocr-cascade's proposed rule (cheap reader, escalate failures, optionally disagreement). A local route's confidence is worth showing as a per-page "check this reading" flag (it ranks the reader's own errors), not as an escalation trigger. Limits: two readers, one escalation route; the Mac's mlx 4-bit log-probabilities come in bf16 steps (1/8 nat), yet rank as well as Sherlock's; the 96 hold only 17-27 poor readings per reader.
- **The seg 2B's P(new)** (`ocr-logprob-seg.md`): AUC 0.76 development / 0.70 test against a wrong gap decision; isotonic on development makes test ECE worse (0.048 to 0.060).

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The owner's own Finder year tags on his 300 documents (W46.date-route-pipeline's owner set); the undated set has no owner year, so there the stamp is compared with the route's explicit text or image year, a relative and not a truth. Nothing new was shown to the owner.
- **Selection.** Every document of the pipeline's two sets with a readable first page and a trace reply; the stamp rows are those whose image reply quotes a written stamped date (where `stamp` or kind `stamped`), which is the condition the step reads. Not chosen by ABBYY text or by the year.
- **Comparison.** Gemini 3.7 Flash's image pass (2,400 px, first two pages) as the route sends it, and the 27B's where its replies are cached; the estimate figure is the same model's step 4 on the same owner set, but on other documents (no document both has a stamp and reaches step 4), so the order rests on two unpaired rates.
- **Noise.** n = 25 stamp documents, Wilson 95% intervals shown: the stamp's lower bound (87%) is above the estimate's upper one (80%), but the two are different documents, so this is not a paired gap. The rerun floor (the same year on 94% of documents between two identical runs, `date-undated.md`) bounds how far either rate can be trusted to repeat.
- **Scope.** Not settled: how a stamp does on a document with no own date at all (the step's real population; the owner rows here mostly carry their own date as well, and a received stamp lags the writing by days, so a December letter stamped in January would be a year late); the stamp's month and day against the page beyond the one firing (`date-full-date.md` scores it with the rest); stamps the image pass misses (it runs only where the text has no year). This is a dating route, not an OCR result, so the both-OCR-sets rule does not apply.

# W46.ocr-logprob — the seg 2B's P(new), calibrated on the development boxes, tested on the test boxes

Generated by `scripts/segbench/ocr_logprob.py seg` from the cached FarmShare answers of `fs-seg-2b` (Qwen3.5 2B fine-tuned on the owner's corpus, the app's local segmenter; `seg-students.md`). No calls, no money. P(new) is the model's stated confidence in its JSON reply, averaged over the windows that judged the gap (`method_window.combine`), not a token probability. Photo gaps and the owner's unsure gaps are left out, as in `score.risk_coverage`: an unsure gap has no known label, so it is neither an agreement nor an error.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The owner's ruled boxes: development Dean, Deaver and Herrnstein, test Stanton and RG165; Stanton carries the owner's 2026-10-31 segmentation-sheet rulings (W46.seg-truth-rescore). The public boxes are agent-audited truths with the owner's rulings on their disputed gaps folded in: Lincoln (`5ba5a0e67380…`: 5 gaps owner-ruled, unsure 76|77, 106|107, 230|231); CREST (`c7a0cfcac896…`: 6 gaps owner-ruled, unsure 45|46, 87|88, 93|94, 110|111). Their other gaps remain agent-audited. Unsure gaps are out of every boundary count; segment figures (pages exact, PQ) are the conditional reading, the route's own call at an unsure gap, so they are not one fixed truth across routes (W46.seg-unsure-mask, `score.py`). Here the two RG165 unsure gaps (79|80, 99|100) are out of every count above, numerators and denominators alike (W46.p15-score-hygiene; before it they were scored with the label the truth file carries there, which the owner left unsure: 315 test gaps, 27 wrong).
- **Selection.** Every document gap of the five owner-ruled boxes; the split into development and test is the bench's fixed one (`data.DEV`, `data.TEST`), not chosen by this route.
- **Comparison.** One route, the 2B, against itself: raw P(new) against its isotonic calibration fitted on the development gaps alone and applied once to the test gaps. Same cached answers, same gaps, for both.
- **Noise.** No rerun floor and no interval: one set of cached answers. The test side has 313 gaps and 25 wrong decisions (Stanton 24, RG165 1), so a per-box AUC on a handful of errors (RG165) says almost nothing, and a Brier or ECE gap of a few thousandths is inside what one more box could move.
- **Scope.** Whether the 2B's stated confidence ranks its own errors on these boxes, and whether isotonic calibration improves its probabilities there. It does not measure OCR, other segmenters, page-level exactness or any route ranking; segment-level figures elsewhere are conditional at unsure gaps (P15-1, W46.seg-unsure-mask).

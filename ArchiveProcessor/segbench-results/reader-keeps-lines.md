## Measurement card

- **Truth.** No text truth is scored. The yardstick is kraken blla's saved line detection, the detector the hand route ships, on the placement truth's audited owner hand pages; the truth's own line count (drawn lines, the owner's hand-line rulings folded in) is shown beside it as a check that blla is not the reason for a ratio.
- **Selection.** All audited owner hand pages of the placement truth; no reader, detector or ABBYY text chose them. Readers: every stage 04 row with a hand figure.
- **Comparison.** Every reader's saved reading of the same pages (the original-scale panel image, its own run settings), cleaned the same way. A page a reader left empty is left out of its median, so `pages` can be under the set.
- **Noise.** A count of saved text, deterministic. The flag sits on a median of up to 20 pages; a reader whose median is within 0.1 of 0.7 is close and is said to be.
- **Scope.** Says whether a reader keeps line breaks, not whether its lines are placed well (that is the line pass score, place-hands-lines.md, measured for three readings); not Bentham or other collections; a reader's prompt can change its line behaviour, so the flag is for the reading the table scores.

# The placement truth's typed lines against blla and PP-OCRv6 (W46.fix-place-truth-lines)

Generated by `scripts/segbench/place_truth_lines.py`. The measurement audit's OCR-10 (`execution-plans/processing-review/11-measurement-audit.md`): the typed truth's lines are largely Apple Vision's, Vision is scored on them, and since 2026-10-29 the line pass rate (a drag from a line's first truth word to its last returns exactly its words) decides the reading copy. No model read a page here and no money was spent: kraken 7.1.1's blla ran on the Mac's CPU, PP-OCRv6 medium det's polygons are the pinned FarmShare run's, and no Claude subagent drew or redrew anything (owner, 2026-10-30).

## Where the typed lines come from

Of the 2,015 typed truth lines on the 40 owner-typed pages, **1,028 (51%) are Vision-derived** (status `drafted`: Vision's lines, its word boxes trimmed to the ink) and **987 (49%) were drawn** by Claude Opus 5.5 subagents on the 20 pages `place_truth.py` redrew; 7 more are text with no geometry. By scored words: 8,283 (59%) lie on Vision-derived lines, 5,824 on drawn ones. A drawn line can still hold Vision's word boxes (p044, p087, p088, p097, p099 kept many), so the word-box count the audit quotes (10,539 of 14,124 Vision's) is not the line count.

## Where the detectors group differently

Each scored truth word goes to the detector line whose polygon holds its centre (the nearest baseline when several do, else a baseline within 0.6 of the word's height). PP-OCRv6's pieces on one row are joined across gaps up to 0.5 of the row's height, so a gutter or a tab stop stays a break. A **split** is one truth line spread over two or more detector lines (a detector line counts when it holds two of the line's words, or only that line's); a **join** is one detector line holding at least half of each of two or more truth lines.

## The owner sheet

97 typed places in 26 questions (places of one kind on one page share a question, up to 6 at a time, so a table of like rows is answered once), then 5 hand pages (p046, p049, p052, p086, p096: seed 20261030, among the 20 the audit passed) with their 128 drawn lines; about 26 minutes at the script's review rates (an estimate). Blind: every typed place asks whether the boxed text is one line or breaks at the dashed marks, and the truth's answer is "one line" for a split place and "breaks" for a join place, so the question does not tell which the truth holds. On a hand page the owner marks each wrong line. The sheet and its key are private, under `ocr-truth/place-lines/`; `place_truth_lines.py fold ANSWERS.json` writes rulings.json beside them. W46.place-print and W46.place-geom fold the answers as their first step (gate W46.place-lines-owner-ok); until then their line pass rates are reported apart on Vision-derived and drawn lines.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The placement truth's typed lines as `place_truth.py` left them: Vision's lines on the drafted pages, Claude Opus 5.5 subagents' on the drawn ones, audited by Claude Sonnet 5.5; the owner has not checked them. This item checks them; it scores no route.
- **Selection.** All 40 owner-typed pages. Places are every line both detectors group differently, not a sample; the hand pages are a seeded draw. Neither detector is Vision or a relative of it, and no ABBYY text is read.
- **Comparison.** Both detectors see the full truth images (PP-OCRv6 a 2400-pixel copy, scaled up). blla is a baseline segmenter trained on historical documents, PP-OCRv6 det a scene-and-document text detector; their agreement is the filter.
- **Noise.** No rerun floor applies (both are deterministic). The grouping rules' thresholds (0.5 join gap, 0.6 grab, break slack 1 word) move single places; requiring both detectors keeps one detector's fragmenting off the sheet.
- **Scope.** A truth line that both detectors group the same wrong way is not found; the 5 hand pages sample the drawn hand lines, they do not clear the other 15. The sheet rules on grouping, not on where a box or baseline lies on a typed page.

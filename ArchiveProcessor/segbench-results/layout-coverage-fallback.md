# W46.layout-newspapers — newspaper coverage and the fill floor

Generated by `scripts/segbench/layout_coverage_fallback.py` from existing numerical caches. No model, image decode, transcription scoring, network or paid call.

The **95% floor is a conservative engineering policy**, not a measured accuracy optimum. It asks for a whole-page fill when the detector crops cover under 95% of the union of Vision's line boxes. The app also fills whenever a Vision line crosses its first crop's boundary, or Vision is unavailable. These are geometry checks, not measurements of ink or OCR accuracy.

## Inputs and method

All 15 L9 newspaper scans and their 8,135 cached Vision line boxes. PP-DocLayoutV3: its existing 1,024px / confidence 0.5 detection, all labels kept, pictures included. The coverage sweep includes every rectangle. The app partition keeps native detector order and omits only crops whose pixels are already covered by earlier crops.

The detector's raw `[y0,x0,y1,x1] / 1000` output was already converted by `regions.boxes_pp` into the cache's top-left `[x0,y0,x1,y1]` fractions. Vision's cached rectangles also have a top-left origin. The script does not flip either again.

Actual crops use `regions_cluster.pixel_box`: 0.004 page padding on each edge, rounded to pixels (nearest, ties to even), minimum 32px per side, clamped to the page. The numerator is the area of the intersection of the union of those crop rectangles with the union of the Vision line rectangles; the denominator is the Vision union's area. Exact rectangle union avoids counting overlapping lines or regions twice.

| Detector rectangles | 76.69% | 95.60% | 92.50% | 99.48% |

## App partition and boundary guard

Under the actual crop union, 7,645 Vision boxes are fully covered, 252 are partially covered and 238 have no covered area. Partial union coverage is any share strictly between 0 and 1 (1e-9 numerical tolerance). It does not catch a line split between two crops whose union covers the complete line, so the app uses the stronger first-owner rule.

In native detector order, the first crop intersecting a line owns its pixels. If that crop does not contain the complete line, the entire line moves to the whole-page fill: it is whitened in every region and restored in the fill. Every transitively intersecting Vision line moves with it, avoiding duplication where line rectangles overlap. Earlier crop rectangles are whitened in later regions. A crop already entirely covered by earlier crops is omitted (half a pixel of area tolerance, as the app). No text deduplication is used.

| Fill condition, app partition | Pages |
|---|---:|
| Coverage below 95% | 7/15 |
| Boundary guard | 15/15 |
| Both coverage and boundary guard | 7/15 |
| Additional pages from boundary guard alone | 8/15 |
| Vision unavailable | 0/15 |
| Any fill condition | 15/15 |

The first-owner check marks 2,578 lines initially; transitive overlap adds 2,444, moving 5,022 complete line boxes to fill in all. The app omits 2 redundant region rectangles. These counts simulate the app's partition on cached geometry only. They do not show that a masked fill improves OCR. The guard cannot account for writing Vision missed.

## Limits and provenance

The older 69.4% figure (`layout-photo-hands.json`) is a different measure: dark pixels below 128 at 1,000px long side, excluding a 2% border, with picture labels and nested boxes dropped. Its 6/15 pages below 80% are not a result for Vision coverage or for pictures kept.

No OCR-quality comparison, photographed newspaper measurement, or optimal threshold search was performed. This is a geometry-only check on the same 15 scans (at most 12 items, almost all one collection); it supports transparent threshold behavior and a sensitivity analysis. It does not show the accuracy or cost of an added fill reading.

Inputs beneath `~/Library/Caches/ArchiveSuiteRehearsal` (or `--cache`):

- `layout-photo/pages.jsonl`: L9 membership and page ids only.
- `layout-photo/regions/pp.json`: detector rectangles and page dimensions.
- `layout-order/vision/newsbench__*.json`: line rectangles and page dimensions; text ignored.

The companion JSON contains numeric per-page measures and counts, no transcriptions or pixels.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** None. The reference for coverage is Apple Vision's own line boxes, not a hand-drawn truth of where the writing is, so writing Vision missed is invisible here and a line box may hold no ink.
- **Selection.** All 15 L9 newspaper scans, the bench's fixed set (at most 12 items, almost all one collection); nothing chosen by the detector or by Vision.
- **Comparison.** The detector's raw rectangles against the app's padded pixel crops and its partition, on the same cached detection (PP-DocLayoutV3, 1,024px, confidence 0.5) and the same Vision lines.
- **Noise.** One cached detection, deterministic geometry: no rerun floor and no interval. With 15 pages, one page is 6.7 points of any share above.
- **Scope.** Geometry only: how much of Vision's line area the crops cover and which pages the fill rules would send to a whole-page reading. It does not measure OCR accuracy, the cost of the fill reading, photographed newspapers or the best floor; the partition's OCR evidence is separate (W46.layout-partition-score, W46.layout-partition-fix).

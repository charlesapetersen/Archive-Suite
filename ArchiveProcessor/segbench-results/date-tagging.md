# Date tagging, scored against the owner's Finder tags (W41.date-tag-measure)

Generated by `scripts/segbench/date_tagging.py score`. The app's default tagging call, `TagGenerator.generateTags` (the automatic mode), with its prompt verbatim and its default model `gemini-3.8-flash`, no thinking setting (as the app sends it), on whole corpus documents. Truth is the owner's own tags on the document's first page: a year tag, a `MM Month` tag, a decade tag, `Date Uncertain`.

Documents are runs of page photos with one identity tag set, kept only where the label-clean teacher and the tags agree on every bounding gap, so each is a real document. Sampled round-robin across collections within each truth class (seed 41). Corpus copies of the development and test boxes are left out (Stanton, RG 165, Deaver, Dean, Herrnstein, as `corpus_pairs` sets aside). The text is ABBYY's layer, not the app's own OCR; context is the three neighbouring tag-rule segments at 300 characters each, as the app builds it.

## Measurement card

- **Truth.** The times are the owner's own, logged by the Debug-only FlaggedCheckTimingTestDriver on the app's Check Flagged Boundaries sheet (each focus change and answer, monotonic clock). The answers are scored against the owner's truth for Stanton and RG165, which the owner made earlier: he has seen these pages before, so the times may be lower than a user's on unseen material.
- **Selection.** Every gap the app's Gemini route flags on the two test boxes at the shipped level (prompt g2, Gemini 3.7 Flash's cached gateway answers, rule min (seg-magazine-rule-app.md), level <= 0.965), 92 gaps in run order; nothing chosen by the time it takes or by ABBYY text. 0 pages turned upright by Apple Vision's clear wins; 4 close calls looked at and upright as taken.
- **Comparison.** One reader, one session, one screen (1496x967 points at 2x); the 20 s it replaces was an assumption, not a measurement.
- **Noise.** n as above; the interval is a bootstrap over gaps within this one session. Between-session and between-reader spread are not measured.
- **Scope.** The time to answer a gap the app flagged, on letters and office files (Stanton) and typed government files (RG165). It does not cover the skim after the flagged pass, other readers, or material the reader has not seen.

## Cause

The filter bar's controls need about 860 pt in one row; at 900 pt the content column beside the 210-pt sidebar
has about 680. The overflowing HStack was centred, so it ran about 50 pt past each edge: the sidebar's scroll view
started left of the window and the needs-attention toggle ended past its right edge. Squeezed, the Q3/Q2/Q1 chips
collapsed to "…" pills. Separately, the sidebar's "Smart Folders" header (an HStack with a Spacer) is laid out at
the row's full width but 4 pt in, so it ran 3 pt past its cell.

## Fix

The bar is a `ViewThatFits`: one row when it fits, else two (tags and dates / name, OCR, needs-attention and the
Save/Clear buttons), else three (Save/Clear on their own row, for a sidebar dragged wide). The quality chips are
`fixedSize`. The header has an 8-pt trailing inset.

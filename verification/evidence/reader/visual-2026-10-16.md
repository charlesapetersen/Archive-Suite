## How the tour is built (for the repeat after a fix batch and before `W40.h3`)

`ArchiveReader/macOS/Tests/ArchiveReaderUITests/VisualReviewUITests.swift`. It asserts only that it reached each
surface (a missing trigger is listed at the end of its test, not fatal at once), never how a surface looks.
Read-only on the fixture: every sheet is left with Cancel or Escape, never Done. Shots are cropped to the app's
windows and popovers; menu shots keep the whole screen. Two lessons from building it: a bare
`app.buttons["Cancel"]` matches the Touch Bar copy, which XCUITest will not click; and a row scrolled off screen
makes `clickRow` fall back to a coordinate tap above the window, which lands in the menu bar.

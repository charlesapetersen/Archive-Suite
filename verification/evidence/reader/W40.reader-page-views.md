## Interaction (VM)

`PageViewsUITests` (a two-file selection: ⌥⌘2 shows both files' thumbnails with the file on view marked; a click
moves to the other file; ⌥⌘6 shows both files' pages and hides the sidebar; a tile returns with the sidebar; ⌥⌘1

3 of 3. Screenshots `uitest-w40-page-views-*.png` were read; both views draw their page pictures.

Before the final structure, 2 of 5 runs failed. After a click on the second file's thumbnail, that file was on view
and its heading was marked, but its cell was not outlined. Each file's cells switched from a stand-in branch to a
`ForEach` branch when its page count arrived, both with the same explicit ids. Now one `ForEach` is keyed by a
`PageSlot` (file and page), and the file sections are keyed by URL.

## Found on the way

`PDFThumbnailer` (ArchiveCore, also behind Copy Archive Link's pictures) sized its bitmap from the unturned crop box.
`PDFPage.draw(with:to:)` applies the page's own `/Rotate`, so a quarter-turned page fell mostly outside the bitmap.
A scratch script showed it: a 200×400 page with a bar along its top, set to `/Rotate 90` and drawn on a 200×400
canvas, came out all white. On a 400×200 canvas the bar was on the right. The canvas now takes the turned shape
(`rendersAQuarterTurnedPageWholeAndTurned`).

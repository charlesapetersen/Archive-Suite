# W40.fix-split-animation-wobble — the slide allowance (VM, 2026-10-21)

**Decision: noise, so the rule changed and the apps did not.** While the Reader's sidebar or Notes' folder tree
opens or closes, the split view re-lays out the list every pass. A list row is wider than the visible list
(`lastColumnOnlyAutoresizingStyle`, columns summing past the clip), so its width turning back by a few points
happens at a clipped edge. It lasts one sampled frame, while the same row moves by a sidebar's width. Nobody can
see it. `TransientAnalysis` now has THE SLIDE ALLOWANCE. A geometry turn-back or overshoot in one coordinate is
not a finding when another coordinate of the same element travels at least 40 pt, the offending coordinate itself
travels less than 40 pt, and it strays by at most 1/20 of that travel (10 pt for a 200 pt sidebar). A turn back
in the coordinate that is moving is still a finding, and so is any wobble with no slide.

`TransientCheckSelfTests` covers both sides: wobbles inside the allowance are clean, including the measured Reader
row; an 11 pt wobble on a 200 pt slide, a 6 pt wobble on a 100 pt slide, a wobble with a slide under 40 pt or none,
and a turn back in a moving coordinate (alone or with another one moving) are each a finding.

The intermittent `splitWobble` `KnownIssue` is deleted from both apps' `TransientStateUITests`, so the sidebar and
folder-tree checks are strict again.

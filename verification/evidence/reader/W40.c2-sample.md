## What was made

The corpus walked: 102,478 PDFs in 270 folder groups (first two folder levels), and 98,884 images in the JPEGS tree.
Sampled: 5,000 PDFs, each group's proportional share with at least one each, drawn with a fixed seed. Copied with them:

- 3,571 JPEGS-tree partner files, by Reader's own rule (`JPEGPartnerIndex.resolve`): 422 PDFs mirrored, 3,065
  unique-stem, 43 ambiguous-stem (every candidate is copied, so they stay ambiguous), 1,470 with no partner;
- 172 main-tree images that share a sampled PDF's stem.

8,743 files, 8.97 GiB. 5,597 carry a Finder-tag xattr. The copy keeps the two-tree layout (`Archival Photos/` and
`Archival Photos JPEGS/`), so Reader is granted the sample's folder as it is granted the real parent.

## Found while making it

- **`ditto` does not clone on APFS.** The plan said `ditto`. A 50-PDF trial with `ditto` used the full 0.35 GiB.
  `cp -c -p` (clonefile(2)) used 24 KiB for a 40 MB PDF, with the same bytes, mtime and xattrs. With 12 GiB free on
  this disk, a full copy of the 9 GiB sample would not have fit above the 8 GiB floor. The script uses `cp -c -p` on
  the corpus's volume and `ditto` only on another one. Clones are copy-on-write, so a write to the sample (W40.d8)
  never reaches the corpus.
- An earlier, killed session left a partly made sample made with full `ditto` copies. Deleting it was refused by the
  session's permissions, so it was moved to `~/Library/Caches/ArchiveSuiteRehearsal/reader-sample.stale-20261014/`
  (6.5 GiB of real disk). It is regenerable cache and can be deleted.

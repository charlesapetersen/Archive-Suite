## Findings

- **No defect in the app reproduced.** Every control did what its ledger row promises, in the simulator.
- **iOS 27 draws a confirmation dialog without its Cancel button** (`clear-dialog-unsent`): the dialog is a popover,
  and a tap outside it cancels. That is the system style, not a bug in the app. The test cancels by tapping outside it.
- **The number pad has no return key**, so the test dismisses it through the Token field. On this screen size Connect
  stays visible and hittable above the pad (`c32-port-numpad-up`, `connect-hittable=true`). An earlier draft of this
  test said the pad covers Connect; that is not reproduced, and nothing is filed. A smaller screen is not checked.
- **Suspect S-040** (`SUSPECTS.md`): the phone's year replaces only the year (and month), keeping the day the OCR read
  from another date (`1962, Day 3` here). This run's segments mixed documents, so it is a suspect, not a bug.
- **Test setup, not a defect:** the feed cycles memo, letter and report through every press, so each segment of tests
  3 to 6 holds different documents and gets one date: in `r5` seven of the nine out2 PDFs are `1958, 07 July, Day 22`
and two `1974, 12 December, Day 3`, whatever each page says. A segment is
  one document by design (`LiveCaptureProcessor.finalizeSegment` tags the group once).
- **Tooling:** a simulator test left DTServiceHub in xcodebuild's session in every run, and after a failed test
  xcodebuild ran a 600 s `simctl diagnose` (`r1`). The script now passes `-collect-test-diagnostics never` and ends
  those helpers once the tests are over (`W40.fix-heavy-run-xcode-helpers` is the real fix in heavy-run.py). After
  each of `r2` to `r5` no DTServiceHub was left and the simulator was shut down.

## What this does not show

The camera (a feed directory stands in for it), a QR scan, a real network dropping (the Mac process is stopped
instead), a locked phone (Home only), and a real device's background limits. Those stay in
`verification/sessions/iphone-1.md` tasks 1 to 8, shortened to the hardware part.

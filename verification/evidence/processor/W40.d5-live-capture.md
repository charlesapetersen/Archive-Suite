## How it runs

The phone reaches the Mac over the companion's own LAN pairing, typed by hand into the Connect screen (host
`10.0.2.2`, the port and the bearer from the app's READY line). `10.0.2.2` is the host's loopback, and the app is in
the VM, so `ArchiveProcessor/scripts/tcp-relay.py` carries each connection to the guest. The direct route to the
VM's `192.168.64.2` fails from this process tree ("No route to host"), so the relay uses the Tart guest agent
(`tart exec -i <vm> nc 127.0.0.1 <port>`). Measured first: host → relay → guest HTTP 200, and the AVD's
`nc 10.0.2.2:<port>` → relay → guest `HTTP/1.0 200 OK`.

`LiveCaptureJourneyUITests` launches the app per phase with `LIVECAPTURE_AUTOSTART=1` and the READY file, but
**without** `LIVECAPTURE_AUTOSKIPTAGS` and `LIVECAPTURE_AUTOFINALIZE`. Everything after the photos arrive is done
through the window: the tag card's fields are typed and clicked, Finish is clicked, the collection is named in the
finalize sheet, and Finalize & move files is clicked. `ArchiveProcessor/scripts/live-capture-journey-phone.sh` waits
for each phase's READY line in the guest, resets the companion, pairs it and drives `android-ui-drive.sh
inject-flow` (inject the photo through the debug seam, tap the shutter, End segment, Skip on the phone). After the
test, `live-capture-journey-record.sh` lists, from outside the runner's sandbox, the filed PDFs' times and tags, the
session folder, and the Trash.

## Run 2 — No tagging (Apple Vision's default): the card's tags are discarded

Settings as a fresh Apple Vision setup has them: Apple Vision, "Use an LLM for text-only judgement" off, so the
tagging mode is forced to No tagging (`Capture/SessionProcessingConfig.swift:289-290`; the panel says "Apple Vision ·
macOS Vision · No tagging").

- Three photos reached the Mac and each produced a tag card (`P-47`). Card 1 was filled by hand: subject
  "W40d5 Dean Alpha" (typed, ⏎ made the chip), Year 1973, Quality "2 — Q2" picked from the menu
  (screenshot `w40d5-card-1-filled.png`: the chip, 1973 and Q2 all shown). Card 2 was skipped. Card 3: subject
  "W40d5 Dean Bravo". Finish became "Finish session (3) →" once all three were processed; the finalize sheet took
  the name "W40d5 Dean live".
- Three PDFs were filed: `W40d5 Dean live/00001 W40d5 Dean live.pdf` … `00003`. **None carries any Finder tag** —
  not the typed subjects, not 1973, not Q2 (decoded both by the runner and by `xattr` outside the sandbox).
- Cause, from the code: with tagging mode `.none` the staging writer skips every Finder-tag write, "including the
  phone boundary" (`Capture/LiveCaptureProcessor.swift:1413-1416`), but the tag card is still shown and Save still
  records the values. The operator types tags that are thrown away without a word. Filed:
  `W40.fix-live-vision-drops-card-tags` (S2).
- **Trash ordering (P-49), measured in the guest:**

  ```

  00001 … .pdf  1792609648     00001-g1f52f943.jpg  1792609758 / 1792609590
  00002 … .pdf  1792609692     00002-gfa1b7101.jpg  1792609758 / 1792609660
  ```

  Every original reached `~/.Trash` (not a hard delete) at 1792609758, after the last PDF was on disk (…751); the
  staging folder `_processed` went with them. The session folder kept only `manifest.json`. This is the code's
  order: files are moved and counted (`LiveCaptureProcessor.swift:2316-2405`), the filed set is persisted
  (`:2033-2037`) and only then `clearFiled` → `trashOrRemove` (`Capture/CaptureSession.swift:772-778`, `:376-380`).

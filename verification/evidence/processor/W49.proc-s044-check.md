## Refutation and scope

A separate read-only adversarial pass found no names/priorities input to `CostEstimator.estimate`, no names
term in `CostEstimate.afterOCR`, and no names row or caveat in `OCRView`. `startProcessing` snapshots the
output collection's settings; `performTaggingPhase` reaches `EntityExtractor.extract` through `EntityRunPolicy` for ranked
names, correspondence, model OCR and sidecars. The extractor makes a separate `LLMTextClient.complete` call.
No comment calls the omission deliberate; the policy itself says a cloud call costs money.

The earlier harness wrote priorities directly and could price a zero-cost stand-in. This check instead saves
the interview and enters actual prices in its fields. Nothing in the estimator or extraction policy was fixed.
**S-044 remains reproduced; W49.proc-fix-s044 is queued.** The fix should retain this run and change its estimate
assertion to require the added names term.

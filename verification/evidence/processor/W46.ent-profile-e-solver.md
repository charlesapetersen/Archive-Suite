# W46.ent-profile-e-solver — profile E's entity pick

Baseline: `beaff1b1` (reported mismatch introduced with the new figures in `9a79dd98`).

## Decision

Keep the shipped solver and route table. Correct the profile E expectation and both profile descriptions.

`ArchiveProcessor/segbench-results/ent-local-shipped.md` scores the shipped tagging prompt on Qwen3.6 27B over
138 correspondence documents' Vision text: owner-rule sender / recipient / place / city F1 **0.66 / 0.68 / 0.56 /
0.71**. The previous 0.62 sender figure used ABBYY text on 82 documents. `ent-feature.md` scores Gemini's
separate entity prompt at **0.69 / 0.88 / 0.80 / 0.88**, costing **$2.37 per 1,000** correspondence documents.

`SettingsSolver.solveEntities` compares sender F1; its `entitiesTie` is 0.05 (ENT-1's few-point noise rule).
The 0.03 sender gap is inside that floor. `defaultPrefer` breaks this tie by cost before time, and
`usdPer1000` prices cluster access at $0, even when a key is also present. Qwen therefore remains the profile E
pick with either a Gemini or OpenRouter key. Gemini's higher recipient and place figures do not override the
sender tie under this rule. Neither measurements nor selection behavior changed.

No paired Qwen-versus-entity-feature result is reported: `ent-local-shipped.md`'s paired Gemini comparison is
against the reverted **tagging** prompt (sender 0.71), not the feature (0.69). Its −0.051 interval must not be
used as a paired feature result. Both could be paired on the same documents; different prompts do not by
themselves make data unpaired. Cluster per-document time remains an estimate and does not decide a cost tie.

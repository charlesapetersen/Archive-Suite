# W49.proc-fix-s041 — the Cost Estimate counts the dating and item-field calls (S-041)

2026-11-20 · `scripts/test-item-fields.sh`, part 6 · $0, key-free, the injected test transport.

## What was missing

`CostEstimator.estimate` had terms for OCR, classification, tagging, collections and rotation. Each tagged document
also makes the dating route's trace call (`DateRoute.run`, on every backend: the measured Gemini 3.7 Flash route on
a remote gateway, the run's own text route elsewhere), its image pass (measured route only, where the text has no
year, or on every document with the Dates card's switch on) and, on the measured route with sidecars on, the
item-field call (`ItemFieldExtractor.extract`, every page's image at 1,600 px). In W40.d1's $0 stand-in run of the
113-photo RG 165 box these were 171 of 341 calls.

## The terms

| call | where it runs | priced at | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| trace | every tagged document | gateway: $0.00220 a call; elsewhere 2,000 input + 300 output tokens on the run's model | `segbench-results/date-route-pipeline.md` "Cost and time" (362 calls); the prompt is ~1,100 characters of template, 3,000 of text, up to 600 of each of six neighbours |
| image pass | measured route, 32% of documents (100% with the switch on) | $0.00284 a call | the same report (366 calls; the 32% is the owner's mix) |
| item fields | measured route, sidecars on | $1.00 / 627 a page | `segbench-results/item-fields-retruth.md` "Spend", drafts/gemini: the same call over 299 documents, 627 pages |

The terms reach every place the estimate is used: Process Files' Cost Estimate box (new "Dating" and "Item fields"
lines), the Settings strip, the run history and the monthly ledger (`RunHistorySnapshot`, fresh and resumed runs),
Live Capture's ledger entry, and `ProcessFilesTestDriver`'s `estimate.txt` (new `dating=` and `itemFields=` fields).
The box's new lines were not drawn in the VM; they are plain text rows, shown only where their figure is above zero.

Documents are the tagging term's ~3 files each. Neither term is batched. The figures are 3.8's list price as a
ceiling; the gateway's own figure was 1.00 times it on the dating calls and lower on the item-field run.

## Checked against W40.d1's runs

- **The stand-in gateway run** (113 photos, gemini-3.7-flash, sidecars on): the estimate was $3.85 with no term for
  the 171 calls. It now adds dating $0.1171 and item fields $0.1802, $4.15 in all, still under that run's $5 cap.
  The stand-in's usage figures are fixed numbers, so its $0.8951 says nothing about the price.
- **The paid OpenRouter run** (Qwen3.8 Flash, no measured route): 195 calls, 113 OCR, 41 tagging and 41 trace. The
  estimate kept OCR $0.1146 and tagging $0.0120 as its `estimate.txt` gave them and adds the trace call at $0.0166,
  $0.1433 in all, against a metered ceiling of $0.1056. The estimator prices 37.7 documents where the run had 41.

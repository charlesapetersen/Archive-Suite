# S-047 · The enclosure relation when the window stage splits its cover

2026-11-21 · Archive Suite baseline `d1ec616d` · Codex `gpt-6.2-sol` · $0.

After a Debug build, run `ArchiveProcessor/scripts/test-segmentation-review.sh` and read the
`9a` and `9b` lines. Section 9 of `SegmentationReviewTestDriver` uses five scratch JPEGs in order:
a folder label, the first and second pages of a letter, a separately marked enclosure, and another
document. The driver supplies the OCR classifications and enclosure flag and replays a window answer.
It confirms the normal review pauses and calls the real `runWindowSegmentationStage`. The control

The Debug build succeeded with the existing VisionOCRKit and AppIntents diagnostics.
Both headless drivers reported `ALL PASS`. The scratch fixtures are generated by the drivers and
cleaned up after use.

## Why the relation drops

`runWindowSegmentationStage` applies the window classifications and the review answers, then calls
`rebuildSegments`. `OCRProcessor.enclosureBaseline` records the immutable OCR grouping and marker.
`DocumentSegmenter.segment(expectedCoverPages:)` accepts the proposed relation only when the current
sequence from the proposed cover to the enclosure exactly matches that baseline. Here the OCR
proposal identifies the letter as pages 2–3. After the split, page 3 is the preceding document and
would be the provisional cover. Its sequence fails the baseline check. The enclosure remains its
own document, with its own date independent of the cover.

The safeguard is deliberate. The W37 implementation commit `5c9cb7d1` says the proposal is dropped
when the cover's pages change. The enclosure marker identifies the original OCR document unit.
After a split, the recorded marker and a boundary vote give no confirmation of which resulting
document actually sent the enclosure. Automatically picking page 2 or page 3 could use a different
document's date. An explicit choice of cover would provide independent evidence for keeping the
relation. The manual tag sheet also accepts a typed Sent With date.

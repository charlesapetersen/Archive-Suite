## S-021 · Android drops the segment completion on a Drive error

The independent Robolectric check cloud-paired a fresh view model, captured a page through
`CaptureViewModel.addDocumentPhoto`, and let the real `DriveClient` and `DriveRelayTransport`
exchange the page, sidecar and matching receipt with the stand-in. End segment opened the tag sheet.
Apply sent Q2, March 1968 and the photographed sequence as the segment completion.

| Drive completion response | Creates after disconnect and re-pair | Completion published | Pending completion on disk |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Success | 1 | Yes | 0 |
| 500 on the first create, success available on any retry | 1 | No | 0 |

A fresh pairing did not make another create. `DriveRelayTransport.upsert` consumed the exception
and `segmentComplete` reported success to `CaptureViewModel.trySendSegmentComplete`, which then
cleared the persisted retry record. The photo had already been receipted. Its completion metadata
was the missing object. The success control checked the sequence, quality, year and month in that object.
The Mac comparison below also observed that, with no completion object, the group was absent
from `completedDocGroups` before Finish. The tag card depends on this state, as traced through
`CaptureSession.pendingTagGroup` and `LiveCaptureView`; this run used headless No tagging.

  assembleDebug testDebugUnitTest --tests '*IndependentRelayEndTest'

## S-038 · Finish files an incomplete relay document

A separate Python peer sent two real fixture JPEGs to the built Mac Processor via the local relay.
The app ran headlessly with on-device Vision, merge on, and all backup, relay and output paths in
a temporary directory. The first case sent both photos and the segment completion before Finish.
The second held the second photo and its completion on the phone side, as the real clients defer
completion until the photos are receipted. The headless session marker called the same
`requestFinish` and `proceedToFinishIfReady` that the Finish button uses.

| Relay delivery | Image sheets filed | PDF pages, including text | Second photo |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Both photos before Finish | 2 | 4 | In the filed PDF |
| Second photo held until Finish filed | 1 | 2 | Receipted after Finish and kept in Backup |

The retained late-arrival run filed in 2.70 seconds. `manifest.json` then listed sequence 2 under the filed
group and the backed-up JPEG existed. The sole filed PDF remained byte-identical. Both local and
Drive relay use `FileRelayReceiver`. Neither relay supplies the pending count to
`CaptureSession.updatePhonePending`, which the LAN heartbeat feeds. The 20-second expiry for an
old heartbeat does not account for this short run, because the relay sent no heartbeat at all. The offline recovery suite passed 300 checks,
including the control in which a fresh pending count holds Finish after the tag card is resolved.

Independent test: [check-relay-finish.py](../../../ArchiveProcessor/scripts/check-relay-finish.py).
Run from the suite root after generating and building the Processor Debug app in `macOS/build/DD`.
Run outside the Codex sandbox so the headless app can start.

```sh
python3 ops/autonomous/heavy-run.py run -- python3 ArchiveProcessor/scripts/check-relay-finish.py
```

The retained independent tests document the baseline failures. When fixing S-021, change the
baseline check to require eventual segment delivery and keep the success control. When fixing
S-038, drive the relay's pending status or equivalent signal, require a held Finish, then release
the delayed photo and require both image sheets in the filed PDF.

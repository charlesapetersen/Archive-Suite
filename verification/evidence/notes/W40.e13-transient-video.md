## What the video saw that the recorder did not

**Notes: the editor lags the inspector on a note change** — the owner's "inspector jitter"
(`W40.fix-notes-inspector-jitter`), most likely. Both runs:

- After "next note (down arrow)": the inspector's Year field already shows the new note ("e.g. 1968", it has no
  year) while the editor still shows the previous note's chip and "Moore on Intel's early egalitarian culture.",
  for 133 ms (run 1, frames 339→342) and 100 ms (run 2, 313→316); then "Notes on the Lovelace paper."
  → `W40.e13-video-note-change-stale-editor.jpg` (four frames, top to bottom).
- After "switch to an empty folder (Inbox)": the inspector says "No note selected" over the previous note's
  body for 183 ms (run 1, frames 657→662) and 33 ms (run 2, one frame).
  → `W40.e13-video-empty-folder-stale-editor.jpg` (five frames).

The recorder passes both because it judges each section alone: the inspector and the editor each go straight from
the old state to the new one, but in different passes. The mixed screen in between is neither settled screen.

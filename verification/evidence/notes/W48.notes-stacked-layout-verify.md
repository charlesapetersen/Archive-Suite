Rule 6: a session that did not build the stacked layout re-ran its checks at HEAD and added its own. All runs in the
Tart VM (`ops/gui/vm-gui-runner.sh notes xcuitest`), scratch fixture only, at `655effca` (the shipping commit

## Expected outcomes, written from the promise before the code was read (rule 3)

- E1. View offers Side by Side and List Above Note. Stacked: the tree stays left, the list is on top of the right
  area, the note below, in the Notes window and the Extracts window; both windows follow one choice.
- E2. A switch, either way, from either window, keeps the selection, the open note's unsaved text and the scroll.
- E3. The horizontal divider drags the list's height, stops at a minimum and a maximum, and the note keeps room.
- E4. The divider height and the layout choice survive a quit and relaunch.
- E5. The divider is an accessibility splitter with a value; VoiceOver meets list, divider, note; Tab crosses it.
- E6. The audit, layout sweep, visual review and writing journey pass stacked, light and dark, as side by side.

## What the builder's tests did not reach: `StackedLayoutVerifyUITests` (new, this item)

- `testExtractBeingEditedSurvivesASwitchFromTheExtractsWindow` (E1, E2): a line typed into the open extract in the
  Extracts window just before each of three switches made with that window key (stacked, side by side, stacked) stayed
  in its editor and reached the extract's file on disk; both windows showed the chosen layout each time. The Extracts
  window's own divider clamps at 220 pt.
- `testDividerGivesWayToAShorterWindowAndComesBack` (E3): in the suite run the window grew to 990 pt and the list's
  drag stopped at **440 pt**, and did not move when dragged past it; up, it stopped at **220 pt**. The window cut to its
  minimum (**759 pt**) left the list 220 pt and the editor 146 pt; grown back to 990 pt, the list returned to 440 pt
  (in the solo run: 846 pt window, 307 pt list, back to 307 pt).
- `testRelaunchRestoresEachLayoutInBothWindows` (E4): stacked with a dragged height, relaunch → stacked, same height,
  the Extracts window stacked; side by side, relaunch → side by side in both windows; back to stacked, the height had
  outlived the spell side by side.

Solo run: 3 of 3 passed (the first attempt failed two of the test's own assumptions, not the app: a drag in a
minimum-height window, where the note's floor leaves the list a 220…220 range by design, and an editor threshold of
150 pt the promise does not make; fixed in the test, then green).

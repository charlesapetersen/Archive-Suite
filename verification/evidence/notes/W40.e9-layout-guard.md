## Calibration (why the text floor is 13 pt)

From the first run's tree, the single-line text heights in the note window: legitimate labels are 13–16 pt (the
sidebar counts and "Location" are the 13s); the clipped list cells are all 12; the only shorter ones are the 1×1
UITest probes, which are skipped as degenerate. An 11 pt floor (the first guess) let the clip through, and that
miss is what set the floor.

## The guard was shown to fail before its passes were trusted

The row clip is a real clipped row, confirmed by the screenshot before the guard was trusted. The guard's first
version passed it, which is what calibrated the floor. With the floor at 13 it fails on every list row. Each suite
also runs `testLayoutGuardCatchesPlantedDefects` (passed here), which feeds the checker synthetic trees: a
squashed text field, text taller than its row, a button past the window edge, one entirely outside it, a scroll
view off the edge, overlapping rows and labels, and an oversized sheet. Each must be reported as its own kind, and
a tidy tree must pass.

Harness exemptions: `an.status.*` (the 1-line probes behind the panes) and `an.editor.test.*` (the UITest button
strip). Both exist only under the UITest launch arguments. Before they were exempted they produced the only other
violations.

# W40.fix-notes-jump-scroll-short — Jump to Source lands on the cited block

2026-11-04, unattended, Tart VM (`ops/gui/vm-gui-runner.sh notes xcuitest`), the scratch fixture store only.
Reproduction from `W40.notes-w7-gui-checks` (runs 5–7 of [`W40.notes-w7-gui-checks.md`](W40.notes-w7-gui-checks.md)):
`NotesW7ChecksUITests.testS3_JumpFromTheExtractsWindowScrollsToALowerBlock`, a jump from the Extracts window to
block 1 of a source note with 100 filler paragraphs above the block and 100 below.

## What was wrong, measured

The probe (`EditorTextView.uiTestInstalledChipGeometry`) now also reports what TextKit draws: the paragraph at the
top and at the bottom of the visible rect, the viewport's character range, and the last `scrollBlockToTop` passes.
The test asserts with them, because the header slot's y alone said "visible" while a screenshot showed paragraph 88
(`W40.fix-notes-jump-scroll-short-ensurelayout-miss.png`, from the first attempt below).

1. **Before:** `scrollRangeToVisible` straight after the new body was applied scrolled by TextKit 2's estimated
   heights and stopped several paragraphs above the block (paragraphs 93–98 shown).
2. **Laying out from the document start to the block, then scrolling to the header's y:** the probe put the header
   inside the view (y 3491, visible 3485…3658), but the viewport drew paragraph 87 there, at an estimated y of
   3470.9 (viewport chars 3567…3813, the header at char 4141). 3 runs of 3 showed paragraphs 88–92.
3. **`relocateViewport(to:)`:** overshot to the end of the note (scroll position 1.0).
4. **Measuring from the viewport's first fragment, laying out on from it to the header:** right in 2 runs of 3; in
   the third the pass read the earlier pass's y back and the view stayed at paragraph 87. The trace showed why:
   the new editor's relayout passes (`relayoutViewportSoon`, queued from `viewDidMoveToWindow`) invalidate the
   whole document after the jump, and the viewport comes back at estimated positions around the scroll offset.

## The fix

- `EditorTextView.scrollBlockToTop(_:)` measures the header from the fragments the viewport has laid out (its
  first fragment, the ones between re-laid so they follow on from it) and scrolls the header to the top of the
  view, repeating until a pass finds the view already there (at most four passes).
- `EditorTextView.whenLayoutSettles(_:)` runs a body once no relayout chain is pending. `MarkdownEditorView`'s
  W7-S3 scroll places the block at once, again when the relayout passes end, and once more a turn later (a resize's
  place restore in `EditorScrollView` had put the view back at the top between passes).
- The same path serves the stale-ordinal top (no range: unchanged, `scrollRangeToVisible` of the start) and
  `NoteEditorPane`'s restore.

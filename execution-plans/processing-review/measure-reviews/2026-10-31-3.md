collections, followed by ten readability exclusions. A document-weighted corpus interval does not follow.

assumption. State the target as the accepted, collection-balanced sample unless valid population weights,
nonresponse treatment and uncertainty are supplied. A model-based posterior interval must be labelled as such,
not silently treated as a design-based corpus confidence interval.

table and `SettingsSolver.segmentationFigure` rank it, and offset ranges use the same estimand. The report says
the unknowns are “left out of every count”, which is false for segment metrics. Counting a flagged unknown as
checking work is reasonable and is not this defect.

**Impact:** the fixed-truth interpretation of the rankings is unsupported. No pick change or size of bias is
claimed: the committed options summary omits the lower/common-support counts. Eight owner truth corrections
stand; there is no need to ask him to invent answers to the two gaps he could not resolve.

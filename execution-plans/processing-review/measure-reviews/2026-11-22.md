### P18-2 · What the local field percentages measure · moderate

`mac_fill_scores` and `stratified` use `item_fields.field_match(..., "ed2")`. Kind matches at the broader Zotero type, a byline earns credit when any author matches, and an issue date can earn credit with a wrong day. The Reader’s `FieldStatusPolicy.itemField` shows 86%, 88% and 89% as estimated precision beside the displayed values. For kind, the same 76 owner-checked fills contain **58 broader matches and 50 exact matches**. These are unweighted counts. The displayed 86% is a weighted estimate. The drafted counts on 292 documents are 252 broader and 236 exact matches.

The owner’s sample has a second limit. `item_fields_more.eligible` excluded fields outside the reference kind. The report counts zero owner-checked bylines or issue dates among 164 such pairs for each field. Switching to the model’s kind raises the gross number of byline fills from 79 to 81 and dates from 70 to 72. At least two of each therefore fall outside the original sampling frame. `stratified` pools by the image prompt’s fill, so these cases can inherit the estimate for cases the owner could sample. Count them separately and give best- and worst-case bounds on their contribution. The independent reviewer identified this gap. Its size beyond those lower bounds is unknown.

The rescore uses saved in-process replies. The app also requires a stopped, non-looping server reply. No 8B image reply and two of 299 4B text replies reached the token cap. The aggregates do not connect those two replies to retained dates. The independent reviewer withdrew a claim that the server changed the reported precision. Label the measured path and check the guards from available metadata. The lower-confidence fill is the owner’s explicit decision.

**Queue:** W46.p18-mac-field-precision. The independent reviewer supported the mismatch in what counts as correct. The new byline measure needs the complete author list, since even the existing “exact” list scorer accepts any one matching author.

### R-1 · W46.date-undated · the page-date truth is the scored model's own reading — weakened (the headline's truth)

- `date-undated.md`: "a date on the document's own pages" is `date_image.py`'s prompt on the first two JPEG pages,
  Gemini 3.7 Flash through the gateway; the scored route is the app's tagging call on the same model through the same
  gateway (the app's default is 3.8, which the gateway does not serve). The owner checked none of the 90 undated
  documents. The card says so ("relative to a Gemini reader, not to a human").
- How good that reader is where the owner has ruled (`date-image.md` §"Against the owner's own reading"): on the 27
  dating cases the owner answered as a date on the page, Gemini found the owner's year for 21. Three of its six misses
  (d06, d18, d20) are documents whose tag differs from the year the owner read on the page, and the model quoted the
  page's year on 3 of 4 such cases; one (a30) the owner called illegible. So of 26 legible page dates the pass missed
  2 outright (a06, stamped; a32, handwritten) and a third if a08 counts (the owner read 1985 on the page, Gemini gave
  the tag's 1984): 8-12%, roughly one in ten, on a small sample [RC: corrected from "2 of 23-24"]; how often it quotes
  a date that is not there was never checked for Gemini (the 27B's 24 quotes were eye-checked before the 2026-10-30 rule, 23 real,
  none invented).
- Consequence for the headline: a **false year** is a year without Date Uncertain where the pass found no own date or a
  different one. A page date the pass misses turns a correct app year into a counted false one, so the 13% (12 of 90,
  Wilson 8-22) leans high; a date the pass invents and the app shares, both being Gemini, would hide a false year. The
  two readings agreeing on 39 of 39 documents where the pass found a date is what either a correct pair or a shared
  habit would produce, and by construction all 12 false-year documents are among the 51 where the pass found nothing.
  The audit's rule (README §"When a truth needs the owner", case 2: a drafter or close relative is scored) says this
  truth cannot carry a verdict alone; the verdict here is a measurement Phase 4 will use to decide the app's behaviour
  on undated documents.
- **Fix:** `W46.date-undated-sheet` and `W46.date-undated-fold` (section 3): the smallest owner pass, the 12 false-year
  documents plus 8 controls drawn where the app's year equals the page's, blind, every date-like string in Vision's
  text boxed on all 20 (boxing only the controls' quoted dates would tell the two groups apart; the item's upright
  page copies went to the Trash and are re-rendered; its Vision text has no word boxes, so `dewarp.swift lines`
  supplies them), about 10 minutes, with its own gate; the fold reports the false-year rate on owner-ruled truth beside
  the 13%, and the pass's miss and invention counts on the 20. Until then the figure is read as "against a Gemini image
  reading", as the card says, and `W46.review-phase4` waits on the fold.
- What stands: the measure itself (the app gives a year on 90 of 90 undated documents; Date Uncertain on 49%), the
  rerun floor (the same year 94%, the same Date Uncertain 98%, read with the gateway's no-cache field), the
  pool-weighted W41 row (89.2% exact, 98.0% within one; recomputed from the strata in `date-tagging.md`), and the DAT-7
  framing. The 10 documents dropped for want of a confirmed JPEG are a convenience exclusion the report states.

### R-2 · regions-orderfree-20261030 · a 0.18-point gap called a loss — minor, corrected (f26f4eaf)

- The report said Gemini's tiles on typed pages with no table or clipping "cost a fifth of a point (-0.18 (-0.37 to
  -0.02)), small, but its interval stays below 0"; commit b62732ca's body: "not a tie order-free". Hours later
  `ocr-rerun-noise.md` published the rule: a gap on the 96 is a tie when under twice the larger rerun range of the
  routes compared (Gemini 3.7 Flash: 0.49 points) or when its paired interval crosses 0; both tests apply. 0.18 is a
  tie by the first test. The rerun range was measured in NES and this gap is in bowF1 on a 41-page group (per kind,
  the kind's own range applies: typewritten 0.21, printed 0.12, twice either still above 0.18); the rule lends a
  route's range until one is measured, and no bowF1 rerun spread exists, so the tie reading is the conservative one.
- Nothing changes downstream: whole-page reading stays the default on every interval that matters (-7.5 and -2.4 on
  the 96). The sentence and the card's Noise line are corrected with a dated note in the report and in
  `corpus/regions_orderfree.py`, which writes it, so a regeneration keeps the correction.

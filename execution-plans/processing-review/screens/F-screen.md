# Strand F screen: placing OCR text on the page image

Scope: an invisible, selectable, searchable text layer aligned word by word or line by line on the page image, instead of a separate text page. Written 2026-10-20.

## 3. READ shortlist (ranked)

1. **local-llm-pdf-ocr (row 1).** It is the same architecture as our sibling project: line boxes from a detector, text from any model, monotonic DP alignment, invisible text scaled to box width. Read its cost function, the two reading-order passes, and the fallbacks. Test: on 50 of our pages, hold Apple Vision lines as the geometry, align olmOCR or a frontier model's text, and score the share of lines whose aligned text matches the Vision line text within 10% edit distance, plus a hand check of 20 pages for hyphenation and column order.
2. **Kraken forced alignment plus CTC alignment (rows 13, 15).** These place known text onto handwriting without trusting the VLM's order. Test: on 30 handwritten pages with a good transcript, compare placement from kraken and from a CTC DP against the character-count aligner, scored with the READ-BAD coverage measure on hand-drawn line boxes.
3. **GutenOCR (row 22).** A model that emits line or word boxes by prompt with published box metrics. Test: run the 3B model locally on typed and handwritten pages, measure detection F1@0.5 against hand-drawn boxes, and compare with Apple Vision lines.
4. **Apple Vision per-word boxes (row 33).** It is the geometry source we already have. Settle whether boundingBox(for:) gives true word boxes at accurate level on the current macOS, and how it behaves on handwriting. Test: 100 typed lines, compare per-word boxes with hand-drawn ones at IoU 0.5 and 0.7.
5. **Orli (row 12).** Baselines with reading order from one model, aimed at historical layouts. Test: replace Apple Vision lines with Orli lines on the 895-page set and on skewed photographs, and count lines found per page against hand counts.
6. **OCRmyPDF fpdf2 and sandwich renderers with the viewer notes (rows 3, 5, 55, 56).** The text layer is only useful if Preview, Acrobat, PDF.js and Spotlight select and search it. Test: build one page three ways (line-scaled, word-scaled, glyphless monospaced), open in Preview, Acrobat Reader, Chrome and PDF.js, and record selection extent, search-hit highlight and copy output.
7. **READ-BAD coverage metric (row 38).** It is a placement score that needs no binarisation and handles skew. Test: adapt it to score the aligned layer's line boxes against hand-drawn baselines on a 40-page set.
8. **OCR-D dewarping note with UVDoc or the Zucker model (rows 46-48).** Phone photographs are dewarped; the layer must land on the original or on the stored dewarped image. Test: decide between embedding the dewarped image (the OCR-D workaround) and mapping boxes back through the stored grid, and measure box error in pixels on 20 curved pages.

## 4. Datasets and benchmarks we could reuse

- **READ-BAD / cBAD (rows 38, 39):** 2036 archival pages with baselines; closest to archival photographs of European handwriting and print; licence not seen. Reuse the metric more than the data.
- **Bullinger corrected subset (row 15):** 100 manually corrected pages of 16th-century letters with line alignment; paper CC BY 4.0; data under the nntp repository. Handwriting only, not US 20th-century.
- **Churro-DS (row 19):** 99,491 pages, text per page only, no boxes; useful for recognition, not placement; licence unknown.
- **olmOCR-bench (row 28):** unit-test benchmark with an OldScan category; no box ground truth; licence to check on the dataset card.
- **OmniDocBench (via rows 26, 27):** https://github.com/opendatalab/OmniDocBench (S), region-level layout boxes on modern documents; not archival.
- **Line-level English page set (row 11):** 251 annotated page images; licence unknown.
- **Our own ground truth:** none of the public sets match typed US memos photographed in boxes. A 40 to 60 page hand-boxed set in PAGE-XML (Aletheia from PRImA, row 44) is the realistic route, with the 895-page public set supplying handwriting and older print.
- **Word-level sets named in a search summary, not individually fetched:** George Washington papers (4860 segmented words), Esposalles, CFRAMUZ. Licences and box availability unconfirmed.

## 5. Open questions for the deep read

1. Does Apple Vision at .accurate give usable per-word boxes through boundingBox(for:), and what fraction of lines on our typed material have word boxes within IoU 0.5 of hand boxes? Is line-level placement enough for selection and search in Preview?
2. How accurate is line-level alignment of position-free VLM text, by model, on typed versus handwritten pages? The local-llm-pdf-ocr project publishes no figures; we need our own error rate and a rule for pages where the aligner should give up and fall back to per-line recognition.
3. When a line is aligned, is word-level placement inside the line better done by proportional character count (what row 1 does), by recogniser-based forced alignment (kraken, CTC), or by Apple Vision's own word boxes? What is the selection error in each?
4. Which viewers honour a horizontal scale (Tz) or per-word spacing: Preview, Acrobat, PDF.js, Chrome, Spotlight indexing? The OCRmyPDF docs say Preview and PDF.js can run words together on sandwich output; this is not yet tested on our output.
5. How do we handle dewarped pages: store the dewarp grid and map boxes to the original photo, or ship the dewarped image? OCR-D says PAGE-XML cannot record the transform; check ALTO and our own PDF choice.
6. PDF/A and accessibility: what do PDF/A-2 and PDF/UA require of an OCR layer (embedded font, ToUnicode, tag structure, language, reading order)? Nothing authoritative was found here; check ISO 19005, ISO 14289 and a veraPDF run on a sample.
7. Hyphenation, column order and marginalia: Text2Image and the Bullinger study show hyphenated words get dropped at line ends; how does our aligner treat them, and does a page with two columns or a stamp cause row-major versus column-major errors?
8. Handwriting: do any of the VLMs here give trustworthy line positions on handwriting, or must lines come from a detector (Surya, Orli, kraken) in every case? No source found measures this.
9. Licence fit: Surya weights restrict commercial use above a revenue threshold; which line detector is clean for an app we distribute (Orli, kraken, PaddleOCR, docTR to be confirmed)?
10. What placement metric do we adopt: READ-BAD coverage on line baselines, detection F1 at IoU 0.5 and 0.7 as in GutenOCR and PLATTER, or a selection-based test on the rendered PDF? The rendered-PDF test is closest to what the user feels.

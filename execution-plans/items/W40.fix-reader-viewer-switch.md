# W40.fix-reader-viewer-switch — session notes

## Measured 2026-11-11 (host, -O probe on 5 real sample PDFs, read only)
- `page.copy()` costs nothing (0.0 ms) and does NOT decode: a copy shares its page's decode cache (a copy's
  re-draw 1–7 ms against 147–177 ms cold). The item's premise that the copy forces a full decode is wrong.
- The cache holds one resolution only: a warm draw at 900×1200 left 450×600 and 1800×2400 as slow as cold
  (also true when the warm draw ran on a background queue). So warming cannot serve PDFView's tiles; a
  prerendered bitmap shown as an overlay (the preview's `PlaceholderPageOverlay`) can.
- JPEG partner: `NSImage(contentsOf:)` + `PDFPage(image:)` 16–17 ms on the main actor per switch (44+75 ms
  the first time); first draw at 900×1200 20–38 ms.
- `JPEGPartnerIndex.resolve` scanned every candidate twice and called `CorpusWalker.discoveredPathPrefix` per
  candidate; `DocumentViewerModel` scanned `candidates` twice more.

## Checkpoint 1 (this commit)
- JPEGPartnerIndex: dictionary lookups (path, stem, relative stem) + `candidate(atPath:)`; scan fallback if
  the root now resolves elsewhere. Test agrees with a full scan on 1,200 candidates.
- DocumentViewerModel: `NeighbourCache` — next/previous file and their JPEG pages opened on a background
  queue, stamped by stat(2), handed over finished; the file just left kept; at most 2 held after `retain`.
- Document window: prerendered bitmaps (`PreviewImagePrefetcher.documentWindow`, pane-sized, 4 kept) for
  the open file and both neighbours, shown by the placeholder overlay on the PDF image page.

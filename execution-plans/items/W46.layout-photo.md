## Size and necessity review (owner rule 2026-10-22; read before submitting)

1. **The question and the decision it could change.** Whether DocLayout-YOLO, the detector the app ships as Core
   ML, loses text on photographed pages, against PP-DocLayoutV3, docling-layout-heron and Apple Vision's document
   request (free, already in the OS). It decides the shipped detector and the handwriting rule of the kind-gated
   layout stage (gap audit R1, R2, R7, R8), and whether a quad or UVDoc pre-step helps the layout stage.
2. **Why results in hand do not settle it.** W46.ocr-regions read only YOLO through newspaper-ocr's own layout
   processing (not the app's region sort) and PP-DocLayoutV3; heron and Vision have no reading on any page.
   RT-DocLayout's Table 2 (YOLO 48.74 on skew against 91.66) is by PP's authors, with XY-cut ordering and their
   recogniser; nothing we have checks it. The Mac-only handwriting arm
   (`segbench-results/layout-photo-hands.md`) says which detectors keep the ink on hands, not what a reader then
   reads from those crops.
3. **Size.** Four jobs: one CPU stage, an eight-page pilot (one Real5 page in every condition, every arm), two
   GLM-OCR shards. CROPS_LINE GLM-OCR read W46.ocr-regions' 5,449 crops in 329 s on one card (16.6 a second at
   concurrency 16), so the reading is about READ_HOURS GPU-hours plus server start-ups; the ceiling reserved is
   0.5 + 2 × 3 = 6.5 GPU-hours, against the gap audit's 15-hour estimate (which assumed about 4,000 Real5 images).
   Smaller would not do: Real5 is used at 155 pages, which is every English OmniDocBench v1.5 page whose truth is
   plain text (no table, equation, mask or inline LaTeX), in four of its five folders (Screen-Photography is not a
   condition a box photograph has) rather than all 6,775 images; the bench pages are the 96, L9 and L4 that
   W46.ocr-regions already read whole and on PP regions, so only the three new arms are sent. No paid arm.
   One reader only (GLM-OCR): the region gain matters for small readers (W46.ocr-regions), and it is the reader
   NewsBench and Real5's GLM-OCR row used.
4. **Verdict: useful now.** It is the evidence for which detector the app ships and for the handwriting gate,
   both open decisions in the processing review's profile routes.

## What the scoring session does

Score with `ocr_panel_score` NES as W46.ocr-regions did (regions.py's stitching: regions joined as paragraphs in
the arm's order; no regions read = 0): bench arms beside the existing whole/PP/YOLO-newspaper-ocr readings; Real5
per condition and detector against its own Scanning figure and against whole page, the drop from Scanning set
beside Table 2's (text only; the leaderboard's Overall also averages table TEDS and formula CDM, which our pages
exclude, so the comparison is of drops, not levels). Staged route: whole, YOLO and PP on the quad and UVDoc copies
of Warping and Skew against the raw photographs. Then the bench page (ArchiveOCRBench), the item's DONE entry and
the bench-cache cleanup (`~/Library/Caches/ArchiveSuiteRehearsal/layout-photo`: Real5 frozen copies, about 0.6 GB,
are rebuildable by `layout_photo.py real5-fetch` and `real5-prep`).

## Measured so far (Mac, no reader)

`segbench-results/layout-photo-hands.md` (checkpoint 2). With the app's setup (ONNX of the shipped weights, conf
0.25, the app's region sort) DocLayout-YOLO labels handwriting as a picture: nothing left to read on 72 of 80 L1
pages, 73 of 80 L2 (Bentham) pages, 10 of the 96's 16 handwritten pages and 49 of 80 L4 registers; PP-DocLayoutV3
leaves 5 / 6 / 1 / 1, heron 3 / 13 / 0 / 0 (it labels a picture on nearly every hand page, but keeps text boxes
too), Vision 1 / 4 / 0 / 1. On the L9 newspapers all four keep every page. So the layout gate's handwriting rule
holds for the shipped detector whatever GLM-OCR then reads: YOLO must not run on hands. The ink share is a
pixel-darkness proxy (stains and rules count), so its levels are rough; the page counts are not.
PP-DocLayoutV3 reproduces W46.ocr-regions' boxes exactly on all 274 shared pages.

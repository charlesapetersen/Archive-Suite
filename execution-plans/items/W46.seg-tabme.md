## The review (2026-11-05)

1. **The question, and what it could change.** Our 2B student puts 78.8% of the test boxes' pages in an exactly
   right document, the 27B teacher far less on Lincoln. Is that ceiling the material (photographed, handwritten,
   mixed boxes) or the model? On TABME++, clean scans of 20th-century US business papers with a commercial OCR, the
   field has published figures: a fine-tuned 7B at page F1 0.987 (A05), encoders 0.78-0.83, zero-shot cloud text
   windows at K = 7 0.74-0.86 (A04). If our students score near the fine-tuned decoders here, the 79% is the
   material, and the student can be offered to other users of the kit as it stands (with a card that says what
   material it was measured on). If they score near the encoders or below, the model is the limit, and the kit's
   default for other users' material must be a stronger route or a per-collection adaptation (W46.seg-adapt-lora).
   `W46.review-phase4` waits on this answer.
2. **Why the results in hand do not settle it.** Every student and teacher figure we have is on our own boxes
   (photographs, label photos, the owner's rules) or Lincoln (19th-century manuscripts). The only public anchor in
   hand is doc-split-v1's zero-shot 0.704 on TABME++, a different model. No result separates material from model.
3. **Size.** Seven jobs: stage and two merges on CPU; four GPU jobs of 2,079 K = 7 windows each. From
   seg-window-modality's measured cluster rates (5.0 s a window for the teacher with images, 0.9 s the 4B, 0.5 s the
   2B, 2026-10-29): teacher-it about 2.9 h, teacher-t at most that (text only, unmeasured), 4b-it about 0.5 h, 2b-it
   about 0.3 h, plus about 15 minutes of start-up each: about 7 GPU-hours, ceiling 8 (the jobs' limits sum to
   11.5 h). **Why not smaller:** the test split is already the smallest published set: 6,236 decisions is A04's
   whole comparison and the bootstrap over 2,238 documents gives intervals of a few points; half of it would widen
   them by about 40% for a saving of 3 GPU-hours, and would no longer be A04's set. The 5,000 published test folders
   (about 150,000 page instances, A05's protocol) are the ceiling and are not run. **Why these arms:** the image arm
   for all three models is the students' own input; the teacher's text-only arm is the one cell directly comparable
   with A04's zero-shot text windows (0.863 Gemini, 0.782 Haiku, 0.739 GPT-5.4-mini). No text-only arm for the
   students: they were trained on images and text, so it would measure the training, not the material.
4. **Verdict: useful now.** The owner promoted it for this reason; its answer feeds Phase 4.

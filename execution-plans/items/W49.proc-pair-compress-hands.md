# W49.proc-pair-compress-hands — image size and handwriting

Owner call 21 (2026-11-19, accepted-issues sheet, `p-pair-size`) asks for a smaller
image-and-text pair, with measurements on the owner's handwriting before choosing its default.
Processor currently targets 2.0 MB for the embedded image. The target is approximate. The finished
PDF also carries the transcript and archival metadata.

## Measurement (2026-11-23, f4eb79f0)

The 21 images were scratch copies of the 19 handwritten and 2 mixed pages in the existing local
`ocr-truth/pages.tsv` selection (`kind` column, JPEGs under `ocr-truth/pages/`). The corpus was
outside the run. Each page's Apple Vision reading was generated once and reused for all four
outputs. The temporary Debug measurement used the existing `OutputMeasureTestDriver` to call
`PDFGenerator.generateOutput` with the same source, transcript and standard layer/straightening
options at 2.0, 1.5, 1.0 and 0.5 MB. It passed the known handwritten kind on the 19 pages and
left the app's kind unspecified for the two mixed pages. The production encoder, PDF/A writer
and defaults were unchanged. Build and measurement took the shared heavy lock. Cost $0.

All 84 finished PDFs opened with two pages, the image embedded, and the same extracted page-two
text as the 2.0 MB run on that page. All four settings used the same straightening turns and the
same image-page text-layer routes (19 transcript-only, 2 with aligned text on the image).

## Legibility

Poppler extracted the actual JPEG in page one of each of the 84 finished PDFs. Apple Vision
read each JPEG locally, using the same accurate en-US recognition settings. The text similarity
score is the suite's normalized edit similarity against the existing reference transcript,
on a scale of 0–100. The table averages the 16 handwritten pages with reviewed references.
The two mixed pages are shown separately because parts of their reference readings did not get
the same blind review. The three handwritten pages whose transcripts were based on agreement
(p086, p094, p096) count in the size totals only. The bootstrap intervals resample the 16
handwritten pages in pairs and describe uncertainty within this sample.

| Image target | Hand score | Change vs. current (95% paired interval) | Mixed p009 / p031 | Median long edge retained | Smallest long edge retained |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2.0 MB | 58.11 | — | 57.40 / 75.75 | 100% | 100% |
| 1.5 MB | 57.35 | −0.76 [−3.50, +1.67] | 57.68 / 75.75 | 98% | 84% |
| 1.0 MB | 59.31 | +1.19 [−0.43, +3.15] | 44.24 / 70.15 | 80% | 69% |
| 0.5 MB | 59.43 | +1.32 [−1.13, +4.19] | 37.57 / 76.49 | 57% | 49% |

Each hand-score interval includes zero. Individual pages can move quite differently. On p007,
the score fell 16 points at 1.5 MB and 6 points at 1.0 MB. The 1.5 MB run kept 10 embedded JPEGs
byte-for-byte and wrote 10 smaller PDFs. One PDF (p015) grew by 44 KB, so the approximate target
can still yield a larger file. Both lower targets shrank all 21 PDFs.

Equal-width crops from p007 (faint cursive), p009 (pencil on a note) and p022 (closely spaced
cursive) were inspected. The same areas stay readable in the 1.5 MB crops at this zoom. At 0.5 MB,
the small writing below the main lines on p009 and the thinner stroke edges in p022 look softer.
Vision's text similarity reflects what a machine could recover from the image. It can move
with line order, recognition errors and incidental print. Visual inspection covered three
examples, with no human reading trial across the full set.

The three comparison PNGs (2.2 MB total) are kept locally for the owner's decision under
`~/Library/Caches/ArchiveSuiteRehearsal/W49.proc-pair-compress-hands/`. No private image or
transcript belongs in Git. The test's product-source instrumentation was removed.

## Owner choice and remaining work

**Recommend 1.5 MB.** It saves 8.1% on these pages, retains a median 98% of the long-edge pixels,
and kept the mixed pencil-note readback close to its current score. Choose 1.0 MB if a one-third
storage saving is worth less zoom detail and the weaker readback on the mixed note. The
0.5 MB target saved about two-thirds and showed the most softening in the comparison crops.

Wait for W49.proc-pair-compress-hands-owner-ok before changing the default. After the decision,
apply the selected default to the paired PDF path and verify it at the normal entry points.
Exercise an explicit image-size override and check that pair structure, the transcript,
archival PDF output, and the separately exported image still behave as intended.

## Size and necessity review

1. **Question and consequence.** Does giving Qwen3.6-27B eight or sixteen corrected line images improve its reading
   of another page of that hand, enough to be a useful training-kit alternative to PP-OCRv6 fine-tuning? Also measure
   whether those examples merely transfer across hands. The 0/8/16 conditions have identical target pages and model
   settings; the eight examples are the prefix of the sixteen. These are prompts, not training or a replacement for
   the app's current route. Phase 4 decides how to use the evidence.
2. **Why current results do not answer it.** PP-OCRv6's 20/40-line fine-tunes tied its base and 80 lines gained only
   slightly. That does not measure image/text examples in a large reader's prompt. The filed Gemini arm has not
   produced a result, so it cannot yet settle this. This open model is a kit route for users without gateway access.
3. **Size and pilot.** One GPU job, one 48/80-GB card, 8 CPUs, 64 GB RAM, explicit 90-minute wall ceiling: at most
   **1.5 GPU-hours**. There are **213 requests on 71 pages**: 40 seeded L1 public pages, all 19 handwritten owner
   pages, and 12 held-out pages of the Blackwell training volume, each at 0/8/16 examples. Four extra public
   Blackwell pages from the earlier fine-tune report are not added to the independent same-volume holdout.
   Estimate **0.5–1 GPU-hour**, not a measurement. In the same allocation, two pages per cohort and all three
   conditions form the 24-request pilot. Every pilot reply must end normally and be nonempty. Its measured request
   time projects the whole experiment; above 60 minutes of inference, or any failed/capped reply, the job stops
   before the rest. Startup is bounded at 15 minutes; no duplicate/resubmission is automatic. Pilot and full work
   share the allocation instead of queuing tiny GPU jobs. The job requests no new model/download/environment.
   The pack is tens of MB; copies and repeated model I/O run in the allocation, not the login node. Existing snapshot
   `Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B-FP8` at `e89b16ebf1988b3d6befa7de50abc2d76f26eb09`, existing vLLM 0.31 image.
4. **Useful now.** This is a still-open Phase 4 experiment. The pilot is the smallest balanced test that exercises
   same-hand, folder-proxy, public-transfer and owner-transfer contexts at each example count. A single page or
   prompt length would miss the context/cap problem and give no paired comparison. No 895-page sweep or extra
   model is added. Reconsider nice-to-have status if the Gemini arm settles the kit's question before this runs.

## The readers and their routes

W46.ocr-new-open's frozen collector sends one image and one prompt to an OpenAI-style vLLM server and keeps the text.
None of these three fits it, so each runs in process through its card's own route (`sherlock/new-open-boxes/boxes_run.py`),
greedy, one reading a page, at most 8,192 new tokens, with the open sweep's three-page pilot gate first:

| key | model (ref, pinned at setup) | route | prompt | runtime |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `unlimited-ocr` | `baidu/Unlimited-OCR` (main; the Mac read `8249d20b…` on 2026-10-31), MIT, 3.3 B, 6.7 GB | the card's `model.infer(eval_mode=True)`, gundam mode (base 1024, crops 640), its sliding n-gram block (35 over a 128-token window); infer()'s `max_length` (prompt included) swapped for `max_new_tokens` 8,192 inside its generate() call | `<image>document parsing.` | transformers 4.57.1 (card pin) over the image's torch 2.13 (card: 2.10) |
| `nemotron-parse-2` | `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-Parse-2.0` (main), OpenMDW-1.1, 0.9 B, 3.6 GB | as its `run_transformers_inference.py`: bf16, greedy, repetition penalty 1.1 (its generation_config), `skip_special_tokens=True` | `</s><s><predict_bbox><predict_classes><output_markdown><predict_no_text_in_pic>` | transformers 5.6.1 and the card's pins; vLLM is supported only 0.20-0.26 |
| `granite-docling-258m` | `ibm-granite/granite-docling-258M` (branch `untied`, `55d41aa2…`), Apache-2.0, 258 M | vLLM 0.31 offline `LLM`, as the card's vLLM example; special tokens kept | `Convert this page to docling.` | the image's vLLM, docling-core 2.105.0 in an overlay held to the image's versions |

Reading text (`raw` in each record, what the scorers read as a native reply): Unlimited-OCR's reply with its
`<|ref|>/<|det|>` tags removed; Nemotron's blocks through its own `postprocess_text` (Markdown text, Markdown tables) in
emitted order; granite's DocTags through docling-core's Markdown export, the card's route, which leaves out page
furniture (headers, footers, page numbers). `model_raw` is the reply as written and `boxes` each block's class and box
in the model's own units: Unlimited-OCR 0-999 of the image, Nemotron 0-1 of its padded 1664x2048 canvas (its
`transform_bbox_to_original` maps back; `image_size` is in the record), DocTags 0-500 of the page.

A granite page whose DocTags docling-core cannot convert (a capped reply cut mid-tag) keeps the element texts and
records `parse_error` in its usage, rather than stopping the run. Granite's per-page `wall_s` is its chunk's average
(32 pages read together on vLLM), not a one-at-a-time figure like the transformers routes'.

Granite's context is 8,192 tokens in all, so on vLLM it gets what the context leaves after the prompt (about 7,000
tokens); a page that fills it is a cap.

## Size and necessity review (owner rule 2026-10-22; read before submitting)

1. **The question and the decision it could change.** Two things. (a) W46.place-print's box-emitting arm compares
   these readers' line and block boxes with Vision's on the placement truth; it waits on this item, and nothing else
   produces open-model boxes for it. (b) Whether a box-emitting reader changes a profile's pick on the 96 and the
   895 (answered-only and strict NES, paired intervals against Qwen3.6 27B and LightOnOCR-3 4B), in particular
   granite-docling as the gap audit's R2 16 GB joint layout-and-text alternative and Nemotron-Parse 2.0 as R2 32 GB's.
2. **Why results in hand do not settle it.** Nothing has read a page with Unlimited-OCR or Nemotron-Parse 2.0. The
   Mac arm (below) reads granite-docling on the 96 only; the 895 is where the layouts, columns and hands are, and
   the paired comparison needs the same 895 pages every other open reader read.
3. **Size.** Seven jobs: one CPU setup (three venvs, about 11 GB of weights to `$HF_HOME`, import checks; exits
   before any GPU job if no model is ready), then per model the 895 on one card and the 96 afterok. The in-job
   pilot is the gate: three pages, every one uncapped and non-empty, or the model stops there and its 96 never
   starts. Estimate: granite on vLLM, batched, from the Mac's 2-5 s a page for 1.5 GB, well under an hour for
   both sets; Nemotron (0.9 B, one page at a time in transformers) about 10 s a page, so about 3 GPU-hours;
   Unlimited-OCR (3.3 B, one page at a time) about 20 s a page, about 6 GPU-hours, more if many pages run to the cap.
   About 10 GPU-hours expected; reserved 24 + 4 + 16 + 3 + 4 + 1 = 52 GPU-hours, generous because the two
   transformers routes are unmeasured and a timed-out run must be resubmitted (the runner resumes; a page already read
   is not read again). Smaller would not do: the both-sets rule (owner, 2026-10-20) needs the 96 and the 895, and
   W46.place-print needs boxes on the pages its truth covers (W46.fix-place-truth: 60 owner pages, 20 L1/L2 and
   40 N6 public pages), so both sets are needed for it too. A subset of the 895 holding only those 60 public pages
   would save most of the 895's hours but leave the text comparison with no paired figure against the other open
   readers; the item asks for both, so the 895 runs whole.
4. **Verdict: useful now.** W46.place-print waits on it, and two of the readers are named profile alternatives
   with no measurement.

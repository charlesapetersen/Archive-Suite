## Size-and-necessity review (Sherlock job; owner rule, 2026-10-22)

1. **Question and route.** Can a trained detector find marks on our pages? The marks layer needs one, because the
   layout stage misses marks (measured above: 0/45). The alternative is a whole-page reader (W46.mixed-route). The
   answer chooses the marks layer's detector for the Mac-local profiles (R1/R2, no LLM), and whether R4 and R5
   need a detector at all.
2. **Why results in hand do not settle it.** No trained marks detector has been run on our pages. PP-DocLayoutV3
   is the only detector measured, and it has no mark classes that fire.
3. **Size.** One CPU setup job, a one-epoch GPU pilot and one GPU training job, plus inference on the 32 pages inside the training job.
   YOLO11m at 1,024 px on AnnoPage reduced to its pages with a Handwritten inscription or Stamp box plus an equal
   sample of pages without either, together with SignverOD (2,299 images on Hugging Face). That is about 6-8k images. Estimate
   [I, from Ultralytics' A100 throughput, not a pilot]: about 3 minutes an epoch, 80 epochs, about 4-5
   GPU-hours. The job carries `time=6` (Ultralytics stops at 6 hours), so the ceiling is 6 GPU-hours. Its first
   epoch's time is logged as the pilot figure. Smaller does not do: the full AnnoPage (7,550 pages, 25 classes) is
   already cut to the two classes we need. SignverOD is the only public set with signature, initials, redaction
   and date. Fewer epochs risk an under-trained model whose miss we would misread as "detectors fail". The own-page fine-tune is left out of the first job (sub-step 4), and so are the WGM synthetic crops. They are
   filed only if the public detector misses marks touching print.
4. **Verdict.** Useful now: Phase 4 waits on this item, and the job is small (≤ 6.5 GPU-hours: a 0.5-hour pilot and the 6-hour training, plus one CPU setup job).

## Necessity and size review (Sherlock rule, owner 2026-10-22)

1. **Question and what it could change.** Does an oracle table-region crop really help Qwen3.6-27B
   read handwritten register fields, compared with the whole page and with single cells? The pilot
   said table 4/8, whole 2/8, cell 0/8 exact on one L4 page. If that holds on more fields, a
   table-region crop arm is worth carrying into the cluster and open profiles (R2/R7/R8) and into
   detector work; if it does not, the table arm stops here. No app default changes either way.
2. **Why the results in hand do not settle it.** Eight fields on one page: a two-field difference is
   within what one page's handwriting or one ditto mark can produce. The owner-print page was
   7/8, 7/8, 8/8, so print shows no signal to chase. The parent W46.ocr-regions run has detector
   regions and tiles, no cell boxes and no table-only control.
3. **Size, from the pilot.** The whole frozen bank: 9 pages (3 L4, 6 of the owner's), 72 fields,
   27 of them handwritten L4 (8 before), 45 owner print. Qwen only: 9 whole + 9 table + 72 cell =
   90 requests. GLM-OCR is left out: 0/20 replies followed the instruction. Two jobs: the CPU stage
   (2 CPUs, 4 GB, 10 minutes) and one GPU job (8 CPUs, 64 GB, one 48 or 80 GB GPU, 1-hour limit).
   Measured in the pilot: the Qwen job ran 4 min 53 s for 20 requests, almost all of it server
   start; its 20 requests took 11.7 s in all (whole 2.2 s, table 1.7 s, cell 0.25 s each). Estimate,
   not a measurement: 90 requests add about 1 minute, so about 6–8 minutes and 0.13 GPU-hours.
   Ceiling 1 GPU-hour (the time limit). The two pilot pages are read again rather than reused, so
   every bank receipt shares one runtime and the scorer never mixes jobs; that costs 20 of the 90
   requests (about 12 s) and gives a repeat check of the pilot at temperature 0.
   Smaller would not do: the bank is the frozen set, and the L4 part is what the question is about.
   Larger is not proposed: more L4 pages would need new truth, a separate item if this holds.
4. **Verdict: useful now.** Cheap (well under a GPU-hour), answers the open question the pilot
   raised, and nothing new has to be frozen.

Optional GLM-OCR arm with its native text prompt: **not included, judged not useful now.** Its
native prompt is a different protocol (the instruction is hashed into the freeze), so it would need
a new freeze and scorer, and it would answer a different question (native OCR of a crop, which
W46.ocr-regions already measured with detector crops). Not filed.

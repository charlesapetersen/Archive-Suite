## Drafter-miss audit on the 32 held-out owner-core pages (45 truth marks)

Before using box-matched agreement between the two independent drafters (Gemini 3.7 Flash + Qwen3-VL 8B MLX) as training labels on the 110 non-core pages, we measured both drafters against the 32 detector-evaluable owner-core pages (`reconciled.json`, 45 truth marks):

- **Marks missed by BOTH drafters:** **1 of 45** (`2.2%`) at IoU `< 0.1` (and **1 of 45** at IoU `< 0.3`).
- **Precision of box-matched agreement (`IoU >= 0.30`) on core truth:** **22 of 23** (`95.7%`) hit a verified truth mark (IoU `>= 0.1`).

## Non-core 110-page training dataset (`marks-detect-own-20261127`)

Drawn strictly from the 110 non-core pages of `marks-set/selection.json` (zero overlap with the 40 owner-core pages or the 32 held-out evaluation pages, verified by `marks_detect_own_cluster.py check`):

- **Positive pages (≥1 agreed box at IoU ≥ 0.30):** 60 pages holding **90 agreed boxes** mapped into public YOLO11m's 6 class slots (`0 handwritten`, `1 stamp`, `2 signature`, `4 redaction`; `marks_detect.py` scores class-agnostically).
- **Clean negative pages (both drafters found 0 marks):** 21 pages.
- **Excluded (never used as negatives):** 26 unagreed-only pages (one drafter saw a mark, the other did not — excluded so YOLO does not learn false negatives on real single-drafter marks) and 3 pages missing a parsed draft.
- **Split (seed 46, stratified 85/15):** 69 train images / 12 val images.
- **Starting weights:** `marks-detect-20261104/runs/public/best.pt` (AnnoPage + SignverOD YOLO11m, SHA-256 verified).

## Size and necessity review (owner rule 2026-10-22)

1. **Question and decision.** Given a mark's own box for a second reading pass, LightOnOCR-3 4B reads 24/35 marks vs 16 whole-page and GLM-OCR reads 21/35 vs 13 (`W46.marks-two-pass`, `p = 0.016` / `0.008`), while the public-only YOLO11m (`best.pt`) buys only 0–3 extra marks because of domain shift. Does fine-tuning `best.pt` on our own 81 high-confidence non-core archival pages raise recall on the 32 held-out owner-core pages enough to unlock that two-pass gain?
2. **Why results in hand do not settle it.** No detector has been trained on our own archival scans yet; `marks-detect-20261104` trained strictly on AnnoPage + SignverOD.
3. **Size.** Very small: 69 train + 12 val images at `imgsz=1024`, 1-epoch pilot + 40 fine-tune epochs (`lr0=0.001`) + 32-page prediction. Expected runtime: **~5–10 minutes on one Colab L4 24 GB or A100 80 GB** (`< 0.2 GPU-hours`).
4. **Verdict:** Worth running on Colab now (tiny compute footprint, `$0` real money, directly answers whether an own-domain fine-tune unlocks the measured two-pass reading gain).

## Size and necessity review (execution-plans/sherlock-access.md)

1. **Question and route.** Can one structured call replace the app's four metadata calls on the open cluster model,
   as W49.proc-metadata-consolidate asks of the lead cloud model? The answer decides whether the unified call is
   offered on the cluster/local route (Qwen3.6 27B is the cluster row of the entity, dating and item-field tables)
   or whether that route keeps the separate calls, and it gives the consolidation item an open-weight row, so the
   verdict does not rest on one provider.
2. **Why results in hand do not settle it.** The prompt is new: no model has answered it. The 27B's existing rows
   are separate calls (the tagging prompt on the 138, the date route's trace), so they measure the baseline, not the
   unified call. The Mac cannot hold the 27B (18 GB; item_fields_mac's header).
3. **Size.** One arm, 299 requests, one GPU. Prompts: median 10,809 characters, 90th percentile 18,255, largest
   25,548 (6,324 prompt tokens on the pilot's clipping, about 7,000 at most, under the 32,768 context with the 6,144-token reply cap). By the cluster's earlier
   27B packs (ent-local-27b: 138 requests at 2,048 tokens, 0.3 GPU-hours, most of it the model's load and graph
   capture), the run is minutes of generation after up to 30 minutes of start-up: 0.5 GPU-hours is the ceiling, the
   job's limit 1 hour. Smaller would not do: the 138 alone give no item-field truth on the published kinds,
   clippings or hands, and the item-field fill rule needs its per-kind floors; a reader set or split is not in play
   with one model. The whole 299 is already the smallest set carrying all four truths.
4. **Verdict: useful now.** The owner front-loaded it (2026-11-24) for the long Slurm queue, and
   W49.proc-metadata-consolidate folds its results in.

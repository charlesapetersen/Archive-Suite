# W46.mixed-route — session notes

## What this item measures, as built (session 1, 2026-11-05)

- The set: the 32 detector-eligible pages of the owner's marks core and their 35 owner-reviewed mark texts, the
  set W46.marks-detect's transcription arm read from crops (`marks_read.py`). There is no body-text truth on these
  pages, so the score is the marks: whether a reader given the whole page gets a mark's words, against reading the
  mark's own region. The floor for a whole-page figure is the same score against the other 31 pages' text.
- Per-line kind: no per-line label exists in the owner core, and the Azure flags the item's text names were never
  made (dropped 2026-10-31: no key). A Vision line is labelled by its overlap with the owner's mark boxes
  (MRK-1: truth from the owner core). Signal: kind-signals.swift's ink periodicity, per line.
- The text layer: `mixed_route_layer.swift` writes each page three ways (labelled invisible run at the mark's box,
  FreeText annotation, text page after the image) and checks PDFKit `findString` (the Reader's DocumentFind call),
  placement, selection, `PDFDocument.string` and Spotlight's importer (`mdimport -t`, nothing indexed).
- LAY-2 (detection held fixed per detector): the region arms here are the owner's boxes (oracle) and YOLO11m's,
  both reading only the mark, so no picture filter drops anything. The PP-DocLayoutV3 and DocLayout-YOLO region
  routing of whole pages (body text) is W46.ocr-regions' and W46.layout-photo's, on the 96 (results in hand).
- Mac arms: Apple Vision and LightOnOCR-3 4B 8-bit, whole page and by mark. Cluster arms: GLM-OCR and
  Qwen3.6-27B, whole page and by mark (the 18 GB Mac cannot hold the 27B). Gemini is W46.mixed-route-paid.

## Sherlock pack — size and necessity review (owner rule, 2026-10-22)

Pack `mixed-route-20261105` (`$AGENT_STATE/artifacts/mixed-route-20261105`, 40 MB with the 32 page copies; manifest
`ArchiveProcessor/scripts/segbench/sherlock/packs/W46.mixed-route.json`). Submit:
`bash <pack>/support/mixed-route/submit.sh <pack>`.

1. **The question and what it can change.** On mixed pages, does a whole-page open reader carry the handwritten
   marks into the text, or must the marks be detected and read as regions? It sets the mixed-page default
   (whole page against detector plus crops) and decides whether W46.marks-detect-own (a detector fine-tune) runs.
   The Mac arms answer it for Vision and the 4B; GLM-OCR is the default region reader and Qwen3.6-27B the strongest
   open whole-page reader, and neither runs on this Mac.
2. **Why results in hand do not settle it.** No open reader has read these pages whole; W46.marks-detect read only
   crops, and only with Vision and the 4B.
3. **Size.** Three jobs: a CPU stage, GLM-OCR (one-hour wall; also the pilot), Qwen3.6-27B afterok GLM-OCR
   (two-hour wall). 86 requests a reader: 32 whole pages, 35 owner-box crops, 6 + 13 YOLO11m crops. GLM-OCR read
   W46.ocr-regions' 5,449 crops in 329 s on one card, so the reading is minutes and the model load dominates:
   expected about 0.5 GPU-hours, ceiling 3. Nothing smaller is possible: these are all the owner-reviewed marks.
4. **Verdict: useful now.** It is the settling measurement for the mixed-page route and gates another item.

Possible existing pack on Sherlock: none expected (the page copies are new to this item); the owner's session
should still look for `$FS_ROOT/mixed-route-20261105` before staging.

## Size and necessity review (execution-plans/sherlock-access.md)

1. **Question and route.** The cluster profile's dating route as one pipeline: Qwen3.6 27B for the trace call
   (steps 1 and 4) and the image pass (step 2) on the same documents and Vision 27.2 text as Gemini's row, with the
   undated set's false-year rate and the evidence checker's first off-sample counts for the 27B (measurement review
   P13-5 asks for both models). It can change the cluster profile's dating pick, which today rests on separate step
   rows (`qwen3.6-27b-trace-neigh-vision`, `qwen3.6-27b-image`), and the flag question at W46.date-route-owner-ok
   for that route.
2. **Why results in hand do not settle it.** The 27B's trace replies were made on Vision text of PDF renders (not the
   JPEG originals) for the owner's 300 only; its image replies cover only the 122 ABBYY-chosen documents; it has no
   reply at all on the undated 90, so no false-year rate exists for the cluster route.
3. **Size.** One GPU job of 551 requests. Pilot figures: 344 text prompts took 312 s wall on one L40S (job 49088016,
   0.98 GPU-s a reply at 16 at once); the image pass ran at a median 17.5 s a call at 2,400 px
   (`date-image.md`). About 0.1 GPU-hour for the text half and at most about 1 for the images run one at a time, less
   batched: about 1 GPU-hour. A smaller sample would not do: the false-year rate needs the 90 and the not-in-text
   figure the 91 owner documents, the same documents Gemini was scored on.
4. **Verdict.** Useful now: the cluster profile is a real route for kit users (owner, 2026-10-22), and the job is small.

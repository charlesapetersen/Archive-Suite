## Necessity and size review (2026-10-29)

1. **The question, and what it could change.** A04 (Multi-Split) makes two claims on clean TABME++ scans: window
   size matters little to a strong model and a lot to a weak one, and images add nothing once the neighbouring
   page's text is in view. Our material has a third of its cues in the image (show-through, a small item on a full
   sheet, paper, handwriting) and weaker OCR. On the 2B (the laptop route), the sweep decides the local route's
   window size (now K = 7 for every model) and whether a text-only window is a real route. A text-only 2B window
   skips the vision encoder, and a text-only Gemini window costs a fraction of an image one. The sweep also says how
   to run `W46.seg-window-modality-paid`: if the 2B is flat in K and modality, the paid sweep can shrink to the
   150-page subset. The vote decides whether the local route and the teacher labeller should self-vote, the only
   lift for profile 1 that needs no labels.
2. **Why the results in hand do not settle it.** Every open-model figure is K = 7 with images. The only window-size
   result on our boxes is Gemini's whole folder against 10-page pieces (93.1% against 89.4%, `seg-scorers.md`), and
   no text-only window has run on our boxes at all. No open model has voted over offsets or prompts.
3. **Size.** Six jobs: a CPU stage, two CPU merges (2B, then 4B), and three GPU jobs.
   - 2B: vote 1,260 units (6 runs over Lincoln 261, Stanton 250 and RG165 113 pages), sweep 2,288 (K 3, 5, 7 and 10,
     with images and text-only, on the 551 development pages; 24 requests shared between arms), whole folder 42 (21
     folders, the longest 82 pages). 4B and teacher: the vote's 1,260 each.
   - Timing from FarmShare on these boxes (`FarmShare-20261018/results/seg/public-*`, concurrency 16) and
     seg-context's Sherlock teacher (510 windows in 48 min 51 s): teacher about 5.7 s a window, so 1,260 take about
     2 hours; 4B about 1 s, about 21 minutes; 2B about 0.5 s a 7-page window, the sweep's image arms about 6,300
     page images at its 15 a second. About 3.5 GPU-hours with vLLM start-up.
   - Ceiling 6.0 GPU-hours: teacher 3 h 30, 2B 1 h 30, 4B 1 h.
   - Each GPU job is its own pilot: 4 units first, the full run only if at least 3 parse.
   - Smaller would not do. The Mac runs the 2B at about 18 s a window (gap audit, Stage 7), so the sweep alone would
     take about 11 hours there, and the 4B and teacher do not run on it. The development boxes are the set A04's
     procedure (choose K on a small labelled set) needs and the item names. The teacher is two-thirds of the GPU
     time. It stays because it labels the kit's training rows, so a vote that lifts it improves the labels.
4. **Verdict: useful now.** It sets the local route's window shape and voting, and the size of a paid item.

## What the pack holds

`seg_window_modality.py pack` wrote `/Users/cp1/.local/state/archive-autonomous/artifacts/seg-window-modality-20261029/`
(284 MB, 1,291 frozen files): `freeze.json` (each box's unit count per arm, every file's SHA-256), `images/` (1,175 pages
at 1024 px), one folder per model and part (`2b-vote`, `2b-sweep`, `2b-folder`, `4b-vote`, `teacher-vote`: each a
`windows.jsonl` whose records name their arms, the images they send and their token limit), `support/segbench/` and
`support/job/` (the four scripts, `sh.env`, `bin/`).

- **The baselines are the scored requests, byte for byte.** `pack` refuses unless the K = 7 image arm (development
  boxes) and the vote's offset-0 l1 arm (Lincoln and the test boxes) equal the keys of the earlier FarmShare rows,
  and Lincoln's image bytes equal the scored packs'. Scoring the cached answers through this scorer gives the
  committed figures: development 2B 93.9 / 88.7 / 88.4% (pooled 90.5%), Stanton 73.3%, RG165 94.3%, Lincoln 2B
  34.9%, 4B 46.7%, teacher 39.5%.
- **Fixed before any answer** (the module docstring): K steps (K - 1) // 2, so every gap is judged about twice at
  every K; the folder unit is method_whole's (runs between the truth's label photos, which the development boxes
  are scored with anyway); text-only replaces the one paragraph about the images; the vote is the six runs' majority,
  a 3-3 tie going to the mean P(new). Nothing is chosen on the test boxes.
- **Read the results by these.** The 2B was trained on K = 7 image windows, so the sweep measures that student off
  its training shape, as a kit user changing K would. The second prompt (w1) is method_window's first wording, not
  the one the students were trained on, so a student's w1 runs may be worse alone. The vote reports that apart
  (`vote-offsets-l1`, `vote-prompts-o0`). The 2B server runs at up to 131,072 tokens and 82 images a prompt (its
  folder units), against FarmShare's 20,480 and 7, so its K = 7 arm against the committed rows is also a check that
  the larger server changes nothing.

## What the job answers

One Sherlock submission, three models on the same 339 windows each (1,017 requests), the bench's request
(`method_local.request`: 7-page windows at stride 3, images at 1,024 px, temperature 0, thinking off, the codex prompt),
served as every FarmShare and seg-window-modality row was (vLLM 0.31, one L40S-class card, fp8 KV, 20,480 tokens):

| set | windows a model | gaps it scores | trained-on? |
|---|---:|---|---|
| checkpack: every corpus_pairs window that holds both pages of one of the owner's 80 checkpack gaps (32 folders, 792 pages) | 160 | 80 (6 owner-unsure out): 9 held out (7 new, 2 continuation), 65 in training collections | 9 held out, the rest trained-on |
| Sasha (180 photos, owner-ruled, label tags only) | ~60 | every known gap | held out: no student trained on it |
| Illustrated Review (147), SC0122 (200) | ~119 | known gaps, unsure out | trained-on (owner, 2026-10-29); W46.seg-boxes-score's teacher-and-4B arm |

Models: the Qwen3.5 27B teacher (`fs-qwen3.5-27b`, FP8, the checkpoint every teacher row used), and the fine-tuned 2B
and 4B (`fs-seg-2b`, `fs-seg-4b`), rebuilt from their FarmShare adapters on a CPU node by the merge job
seg-window-modality used (it completed there on 2026-10-30). The 2B on all three boxes also gives seg-boxes-score the
full-precision row beside its Mac 8-bit one.

**No ABBYY text (owner, 2026-10-30).** The students trained on corpus windows carrying ABBYY's text layer. These
windows carry Apple Vision's reading of each JPEG original instead (segbench's `ocr.swift`, the app's call), as every
bench box does and as the app will read. So the checkpack arm measures the students on the app's input, not on their
training input; the report must say so. `pack` refuses a prompt that names ABBYY.

Scoring (`kit_gate_sherlock.py score --pack <Mac artifact> --results <Mac artifact>`): imports each model's answers
into its response cache (call logs untouched: they hold the FarmShare runs), then
- checkpack: a gap's call is the mean P(new) over its covering windows (method_window.combine), against the owner's
  answer; accuracy, false splits and merges by split (held-out 9, training 65, all), and the teacher split;
- boxes: `kit_gate.box_record` per box (known gaps only, pages exact over the documents no unsure gap touches), and
  `kit_gate.teacher_split`;
- writes `segbench-results/seg-kit-gate-sherlock.json`. Folding it into `seg-kit-gate.md` (its "Not yet on the
  owner's 80-gap checkpack or Sasha" line, the measurement card's Scope) and ArchiveOCRBench is the scoring
  session's job, with seg-boxes-score reading the box rows.

## Size and necessity review (owner rule 2026-10-22; read before submitting)

1. **The question and the decision it could change.** Whether the shipped 2B (and the 4B) are accurate on
   owner-ruled gaps they never trained on, and whether their errors there are the teacher's. That is the measurement
   audit's SEG-5: every student figure in hand is agreement with the teacher on agreed labels or a score on boxes the
   students were developed against. It feeds Phase 4's segmentation default (shipped 2B, 4B, or the 27B/cloud) and the
   kit gate's acceptance rule (gap audit item 20), and supplies W46.seg-boxes-score's teacher and 4B rows on the three
   locked boxes so that item does not run the teacher again.
2. **Why results in hand do not settle it.** No route has an answer on any of these windows: the checkpack's windows
   were last sent with ABBYY text (the label-clean teacher run), which the owner has ruled out, and the three locked
   boxes were frozen on 2026-10-29/30, after every Sherlock and FarmShare run.
3. **Size.** Six jobs: CPU stage (pack copy and hash check, base models only if the cache lacks them), teacher on one
   GPU, two CPU merges, the 2B and the 4B on one GPU each. Measured on Sherlock for the same request and models
   (seg-window-modality, 2026-10-30, sacct): teacher 1,260 windows in 1 h 50 min, 4B 1,260 in 22 min, 2B 3,590 in
   25 min, load included. For 339 windows: teacher about 40 min, 4B about 10, 2B about 6, so **about 0.9 GPU-hours
   expected**; time limits 1:30 + 0:45 + 0:45 = **3.0 GPU-hours ceiling**. A smaller set would not do: the
   held-out checkpack is already only 9 gaps, and Sasha is the only held-out owner box, scored whole; the two
   trained-on boxes are seg-boxes-score's, and dropping them would mean a second teacher job later.
4. **Verdict: useful now.** Phase 4 (W46.review-phase4) and W46.seg-boxes-score both wait on it.

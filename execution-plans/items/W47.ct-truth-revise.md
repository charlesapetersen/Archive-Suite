Individuals"), a time period or date, a series title separate from the series number ("Series III" and
"Correspondence"), a collection subtitle, a call or location mark the owner calls other identifying information

**Rules for reading.** Nothing is inferred: a repository or place not written on the label is not filled in
(ac365f0cf581, 65b5101c5a45). "Series" and "Accession" are used only when the label says so ("ACCN" counts);
otherwise the value goes under other identifying information. The container is the box number alone ("Box 34",
not "Box 34 of 201" or "Box #186"). Folder titles are never read off a box label. A mark like "5-9" or "8.5" on a
folder means box 5 folder 9, box 8 folder 5. When a box and its folder disagree on the collection, the box wins,
and a folder's collection title is often a short form. A cut-off title is flagged as cut off.

**Normalisation (product, not truth).** Title case except acronyms; a user-set standard name per repository and
collection ("Harvard Business School, Baker Library"). These are processor features; file them as such, and decide
whether the scoring compares case-folded.

**Open questions for the next session.**
- **Day.** The corpus targets say `"day": null` because the owner's tags carry no day. Mixed with the public set's
  real days, that teaches "null" wherever the corpus style shows. Options: drop `day` from corpus targets and
  mask it out, or keep null and rely on the public set. Decide this when building the mixed set, and measure it.
- **Neighbour leakage.** A training document's context can hold up to 300 characters of a test document that sits
  within three segments of it. That is not removed yet; count it, and drop those training rows if there are many.

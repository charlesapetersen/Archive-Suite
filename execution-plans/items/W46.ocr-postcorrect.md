# W46.ocr-postcorrect

## Sherlock session, 2026-10-22 (owner-led; the engine was parked)

Size and necessity review. The question: whether a correction stage belongs in the OCR cascade, and for whom (C test
6). Nothing has measured it. Size: 232 items x 2 arms = 464 Qwen3.6-27B requests, under a GPU-hour; the band is small by
construction (only pages a cheap reader scored NES 0.70-0.90), so nothing smaller would still cover the four cheap
owner readers and three open routes. Verdict: useful now.

The pack (`postcorrect.py pack`, built on the Mac from `ocr-scorers-pages.csv` and ocr_rescore.py's own loaders):
owner96 band 72 (Apple Vision, LightOnOCR-3 4B MLX, Qwen3-VL 8B, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, all band pages), owner96 easy
40 (the abstention check: up to 10 a route at NES >= 0.97), public895 band 120 (GLM-OCR, LightOnOCR-3 4B, Qwen3-VL 8B,
40 a route spread over L1, L2, L4, L6; seed 46). The 96's images are `ocr-panel-local/images/<page>-original.jpg`,
the files the panel and truth96 read (all 59 match truth99.jsonl's hashes; `ocr-truth/pages` holds different files).
Each item is sent image-plus-text and text-only; the client refuses an image whose hash differs.

On Sherlock in `$SCRATCH/fs-ocr/postcorrect-20261022`: pilot 49139316 (6 requests), full run 49139318 afterok
(`sherlock/postcorrect-job.sh`, Qwen3.6-27B FP8 with the regions run's recorded vLLM arguments, thinking off,
temperature 0). Replies in `out/<id>.json`.

Left for the next session (the engine may do it once results are back): copy `out/` home, and write `postcorrect.py
score`: NES and cMER of the corrected text against truth beside the reading's, the added-words rate, and on the easy
items the share changed at all, which must be near zero; by set, route and arm.

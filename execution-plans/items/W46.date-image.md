# W46.date-image

## Sherlock session, 2026-10-22 (owner-led; the engine was parked): the open cluster arm

Size and necessity review. The question (B test 2): how many explicit dates are on the page and not in the text,
which decides whether an image pass belongs in the dating pipeline and whether a specialist date reader is worth
building. Nothing has measured it. Size: 122 documents x 2 variants = 244 Qwen3.6-27B requests of up to two pages,
about an hour on one GPU. Verdict: useful now. The Gemini arm (paid) waits on W46.gateway-models-ok; the Mac arms
(Qwen3-VL 8B MLX, the look-alike retrieval) and the typeface probe are the item's other sessions.

Images (owner, 2026-10-22): the JPEG originals, never the ABBYY-processed corpus PDFs. `date_image.py pack` takes each
page's JPEG from corpus_pairs.py's pairs.tsv and confirms it with the truth builder's correlation test
(ocr_truth.orient_like_pdf >= 0.65; the 96's known pairs measured 0.69-0.98, non-matching candidates 0.16-0.48), whose
best turn makes it upright; 2,400 px long side. corpus_pairs.py's difference hash is not a usable confirmation (true
pairs spread 8-61 bits). Result: 180 pages from JPEG, 22 rejected as a different photo, 9 documents left with no page
(122 of the 131: A 32, A' 7, B 35, C 31, D 17; D first in the pack).

Variants: the images alone, and the images with the ABBYY text (first 6,000 characters). The reply quotes each date,
its page, place and kind, and the document's own date. On Sherlock in `$SCRATCH/fs-ocr/date-image-20261022`: pilot
49140488 (4 requests), full 49140491 afterok (`sherlock/postcorrect-job.sh`, the same Qwen3.6-27B server).

Left for the next session: copy `out/` home and score: the quoted date against the page (by eye on a sample, or Vision
text of the JPEG), the year against the owner's tag, the share handwritten, stamped, in a letterhead or on page 2, and
image against image+text, by class.
